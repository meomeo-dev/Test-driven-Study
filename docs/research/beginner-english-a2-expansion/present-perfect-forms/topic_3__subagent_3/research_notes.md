## 一、中文母语高频负迁移偏误谱系（Error Taxonomy）

汉语无动词形态屈折，表达“做完了 / 去过”依靠动态助词“了 / 过”，既无助动词 `have / has` 人称变化，也不区分过去式（V2）与过去分词（V3）。叠加先修 `past-irregular` 与 `did-questions` 的规则干扰，A2 学习者集中出现以下 6 类高频错误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移 / 干扰根源 | 典型误句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **混用过去式（V2）或原形（V1）代过去分词（V3）** | 仅熟记先修 V2（`went / saw / wrote`）；误把 `have/has` 当 `did` 后退回原形；规则化错拼 | `*I have went to Hangzhou twice.`<br>`*She has wrote the report.`<br>`*He has finish the slides.`<br>`*They have sended the file.` | `I have been to Hangzhou twice.`<br>`She has written the report.`<br>`He has finished the slides.`<br>`They have sent the file.` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`has / have` 主谓不一致、漏助动词或混用 `do / did`** | 汉语“了 / 过”不随主语变化；缩写 `'ve / 's` 弱读漏写；疑问否定套用 `did / don't` | `*My manager have sent the email.`<br>`*Everyone have arrived.`<br>`*Did you have booked the room?`<br>`*He doesn't have written the note.` | `My manager has sent the email.`<br>`Everyone has arrived.`<br>`Have you booked the room?`<br>`He hasn't written the note.` | **P0** |
| **E3** | **`have been to` vs. `have gone to` 混淆及 `to` 误用** | 汉语“去过”与“去了”共用“去”；直译“我去过东京”为 `*have gone to`；在 `home / there` 前多加 `to` | `*I have gone to Tokyo twice.`<br>`*Where's Clara? — She's been to the bank.`<br>`*He has gone to home.` | `I have been to Tokyo twice.`<br>`Where's Clara? — She's gone to the bank.`<br>`He has gone home.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **缩写 `'s` 误还原为 `is` 与肯定简答误缩写** | `He's / She's` 同时缩 `is` 与 `has` 易错判；将陈述句缩写 `I've / She's` 搬进简答 | 误解 `She's sent it.` 为 `*She is sent it.`<br>`*Have you finished? — Yes, I've.`<br>`*Has he called? — Yes, he called.` | 还原：`She has sent it.`<br>`Yes, I have.` / `No, I haven't.`<br>`Yes, he has.` / `No, he hasn't.` | **P1** |
| **E5** | **过去分词双写、变 `y` 及特殊拼写失误** | 规则重读闭音节漏双写、辅音 + `y` 未改 `i`；不规则词拼写缺字母 | `*She has planed the trip.`<br>`*He has replyed to the client.`<br>`*I have payed by card.`<br>`*We have writen the summary.` | `She has planned the trip.`<br>`He has replied to the client.`<br>`I have paid by card.`<br>`We have written the summary.` | **P1** |
| **E6** | **误配明确已结束过去时间点（前瞻预警）** | 直译“我昨天已经订好票了”，将 `yesterday` 与完成时共现 | `*I have booked the train yesterday.` | `I booked the train yesterday.`（或去时间点：`I have booked the train.`） | **P2** |

### 核心偏误深度纠偏要点（直击 P0 三大顽疾）

1. **纠偏 E1（切断 V2 与 V3 混用，三型分层记不规则动词）**：
   - 衔接第 8 单元已学 12 个不规则动词与日常职场高频词，按形态分三组锁定**头号错题区 A-B-C 型**：
     - **A-B-C 型（V2 ≠ V3，重点攻坚）**：`be–was/were–been`、`go–went–gone/been`、`do–did–done`、`see–saw–seen`、`take–took–taken`、`give–gave–given`、`break–broke–broken`、`write–wrote–written`、`eat–ate–eaten`、`choose–chose–chosen`、`forget–forgot–forgotten`、`speak–spoke–spoken`。
     - **A-B-B 型（V2 = V3，稳固减负）**：`send–sent–sent`、`have–had–had`、`make–made–made`、`find–found–found`、`tell–told–told`、`buy–bought–bought`、`pay–paid–paid`、`leave–left–left`、`meet–met–met`。
     - **A-A-A / A-B-A 型（防乱加 `-ed`）**：`put–put–put`、`read–read–read`（读音 `/riːd/–/red/–/red/`）、`come–came–come`。
   - **槽位铁律**：`did / didn't` 后动词退回原形，而 **`have / has / haven't / hasn't` 后无论肯定、否定还是疑问，永远锁定过去分词（V3）**（❌ `*Has she write the summary?` → ✅ `Has she written the summary?`）。
2. **纠偏 E2（主谓一致与独立助动词原则）**：
   - **主语二分**：`he / she / it`、单数名词（`my manager`）、不可数名词（`the news`）及不定代词（`everyone / someone / nobody`）必配 **`has / 's / hasn't`**；`I / you / we / they` 及复数名词（`Mia and Leo`、`the files`）必配 **`have / 've / haven't`**。
   - **独立助动词**：完成时里的 `have / has` 本身即助动词，变否定直接加 `not`，变疑问直接提前，**绝不借用 `do / does / did`**；同时提醒学习者：`have` 作实义动词（吃、开会、收到）进入完成时会自然出现 **`have had / has had`**（如 `We've had two meetings today.`），并非重复笔误。
3. **纠偏 E3（`have been to` vs. `have gone to` 状态二分）**：
   - 依 *Cambridge Dictionary — English Grammar Today*，唯一判据是**“人现在回来了没有”**：
     - **`have/has been to`**：去过且**已经返回**，常用于谈个人经历或刚办完事回来（`I've been to the supermarket—would you like an apple?`），一、二、三人称皆可用。
     - **`have/has gone to`**：已经出发、**尚未返回**（人在当地或途中），多用于第三人称（`Where's Ben? — He's gone to the clinic.`）；说话双方在场交流过往经历时，绝不可用 `*I have gone to...` 或 `*Have you gone to...`。
     - **地点副词零介词**：后接 `home / there / here / abroad` 时删去 `to`（✅ `She's gone home.` / `Have you been abroad?`）。

## 二、易混辨析矩阵与三步自检法

### 1. 四组核心易混结构辨析

| 辨析维度 | 形式 A | 形式 B | 判别依据与地道对照 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. `been to` vs. `gone to`** | `have/has been to`（去过已回） | `have/has gone to`（去了未回） | 看人是否在场：谈旅行/到访次数或刚归来用 `been to`（*Anna has been to Chengdu twice.*）；找人不在解释去向用 `gone to`（*Sam has gone to the print shop.*）。 |
| **2. `'s` 的两种真身** | `'s + 过去分词` = **`has`**（现在完成时） | `'s + V-ing / 形容词 / 介词短语` = **`is`** | 看紧随词形：*She's **sent** the slides.*（= `She has sent`）vs. *She's **sending** the slides.*（= `She is sending`）。 |
| **3. 疑问否定 `Have/Has + V3` vs. `Did + V1`** | `Have/Has + 主语 + V3` / `haven't + V3` | `Did + 主语 + V1` / `didn't + V1` | `did` 将动词拉回原形（*Did you **write** it yesterday?*）；`have/has` 始终配 V3（*Have you **written** it?*）。 |
| **4. 简答形式规范** | `Yes, I have.` / `Yes, she has.`（完整助动词） | `No, I haven't.` / `No, she hasn't.`（可缩写） | 依 *British Council* 与 *Cambridge Grammar*，肯定简答**禁缩写**（❌ `*Yes, I've.`），且不重复实义动词（❌ `*Yes, I finished.`）。 |

### 2. 学习者“三步纠偏自检法”
1. **看主语选助动词**：三单主语及 `everyone / someone` → `has / hasn't`，其余 → `have / haven't`；核查无漏助动词、无多余 `do / did`。
2. **锁三态查 V3**：先判规则/不规则，再核对第三列过去分词及拼写（尤其警觉 `wrote→written`、`saw→seen`、`ate→eaten`、`forgot→forgotten`、`left` 配地点）；否定疑问同样保持 V3。
3. **查去向与简答**：涉及去某地，已回/谈经历选 `been to`，人在外面选 `gone to`，`home / there / abroad` 去 `to`；肯定简答写全 `Yes, ... have/has.`。

## 三、讲义示范例题（对应 `min_examples: 3`）

### 例题 1：典型错题排雷改错（专治 E1 / E2 / E3 / E4）
- **题干**：找出下列 4 句日常与职场句子中的错误并改正：
  1. `My manager have wrote a short summary for the team.`
  2. `I have gone to Suzhou twice and really like the gardens.`
  3. `Did you have sent the meeting link to the client?`
  4. `Has she finish the budget sheet? — Yes, she's.`
- **参考解答**：
  1. `My manager has written a short summary for the team.`（三单主语用 `has`；`write` 过去分词为双 `t` 的 `written`。）
  2. `I have been to Suzhou twice and really like the gardens.`（说话人谈去过且已返回的经历，用 `been to`。）
  3. `Have you sent the meeting link to the client?`（现在完成时疑问直接提 `Have`，不借 `Did`。）
  4. `Has she finished the budget sheet? — Yes, she has.`（疑问句仍用 V3 `finished`；肯定简答不缩写为 `*she's`。）

### 例题 2：`been to / gone to` 与 `'s` 还原辨析（专治 E3 / E4）
- **题干**：根据语境补全或还原括号中的结构：
  1. `A: "Where is Leo?" B: "He ____ (has been / has gone) to the café downstairs to grab a coffee."`
  2. `Nina ____ (has been / has gone) to the supermarket—look, she bought fresh strawberries for us!`
  3. 写出缩写 `'s` 的完整形式：`a) He's booked two train tickets.` / `b) He's booking the train tickets now.`
- **参考解答**：`1. has gone`（人此刻不在，下楼买咖啡未归）；`2. has been`（已带着草莓回来）；`3. a) He has booked`（后接 V3 表完成），`b) He is booking`（后接 `V-ing` 表正在进行）。

### 例题 3：日常与职场汉译英（整合三态、主谓一致与去向辨析）
- **题干**：将下面两句译为地道英文：
  1. 安娜因公去过成都两次；今天她去客户办公室了，下午五点回来。
  2. 你把报告写完了吗？——是的，我写完了，而且我也把文件发给经理了。
- **参考解答**：
  1. `Anna has been to Chengdu twice for work. Today she has gone to the client's office, and she'll be back at five.`（先 `has been to` 表经历，再 `has gone to` 表未归。）
  2. `Have you written the report? — Yes, I have, and I've sent the file to the manager.`（疑问保持 V3 `written`，肯定简答写全 `Yes, I have.`，搭配 `sent`。）

## 四、听说读写四层训练体系与 10 题蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

本讲 `budget_plan.json` 核定配额为 **10 题、覆盖 7 种题型**（`single_choice` × 2、`multiple_choice` × 1、`cloze` × 2、`reading_comprehension` × 1、`listening` × 1、`translation` × 2、`writing: sentence_making` × 1；`practice_design_min = 32.5`，`planned_item_design_min = 34.5`）。

| 训练层级 | 对应题型与数量 | 聚焦技能 | 核心纠偏与测评目标 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 单点排雷** | `single_choice` × 2 | 语法 + 读 | 精准打击 E1（`wrote/write` 混用）、E2（`have/has` 不一致）与 E3（`has gone` vs. `has been` 未归判别） |
| **L2 语篇综合** | `multiple_choice` × 1、`cloze` × 2 | 读 + 语法 | 5 选 3 综合辨错；两篇连贯短文（日常生活 + 现代职场）交替演练三单/复数/不定代词一致、不规则 V3 与去向辨析 |

### 10 道 v2 题库详细设计（含干扰机制与 TTS / `study_translation` 规范）

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：三单主谓一致 + 不规则过去分词）**
   - **题干**（含空位禁配顶层 TTS）：选出语法完全正确的一项：`"My manager ____ the final report and emailed it to the client."`
   - **选项**（含病句，遵 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组禁配 TTS**；解析内为正确整句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `has written`（三单配 `has`，`write` 过去分词为 `written`）
     - ❌ `have written`（E2：三单主语误用 `have`）
     - ❌ `has wrote`（E1：混用过去式 `wrote` 代过去分词）
     - ❌ `has write`（E1：误用动词原形）

2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：`have gone to` vs. `have been to` 场景判别）**
   - **题干**（含空位禁配顶层 TTS）：补全职场对话：`A: "Is Daniel in his office? I need to ask him about the meeting." B: "No, he ____ to the bank. He'll be back in twenty minutes."`
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `has gone`（人不在办公室且二十分钟后才回，用 `has gone to`）
     - ❌ `has been`（E3：`been to` 表去过已回，与人不在场矛盾）
     - ❌ `have gone`（E2：三单主语 `he` 误配 `have`）
     - ❌ `has went`（E1：误把过去式 `went` 当过去分词）

3. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：多句综合语法与语用诊断，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**（纯中文无 TTS）：以下哪些日常与职场英语句子**语法正确且语义自然**？（多选）
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `Have you seen my laptop charger anywhere?`（疑问提 `Have` + V3 `seen`）
     - ✅ `Claire has been to Nanjing twice, so she knows the city well.`（三单 `has` + `been to` 谈经历）
     - ✅ `We haven't chosen a restaurant for Friday's dinner.`（否定 `haven't` + V3 `chosen`）
     - ❌ `I have gone to Kyoto twice and loved the local food.`（E3：自述去过经历误用 `gone to`）
     - ❌ `Did your teammate has finished the presentation slides?`（E2：`did` 与 `has` 助动词杂糅）

4. **`cloze` #1（L2：周末日常与出行生活完形，5 空）**

   - **五空诊断点**（全组禁配选项 TTS）：`{{1}}` `have`（✅ 复数主语）/ `has` / `are` / `did`；`{{2}}` `has`（✅ 三单 + `gone to`）/ `have` / `is` / `does`；`{{3}}` `eaten`（✅ `eat–ate–eaten`）/ `ate` / `eat` / `eating`；`{{4}}` `sent`（✅ `send–sent–sent`）/ `send` / `sended` / `sending`；`{{5}}` `left`（✅ `leave–left–left`，物 + 地点用地道 `leave`）/ `leave` / `leaved` / `forgot`。
   - **复盘语音 `passage_tts`**（仅背题与复盘下发）：`{"voice": "stella", "text": "Maya and her sister have been to Suzhou three times, so they know the best local cafés there. 5. **`cloze` #2（L2：现代职场客户汇报准备完形，5 空）**
   - **短文**（约 82 词，原文切片禁挂内联 `tts`）：
     > Our design team is getting ready for tomorrow's client presentation. Everyone {{1}} worked hard this week. Leo has {{2}} the welcome speech, and Nina has {{3}} the latest charts to the shared folder.    - **五空诊断点**（全组禁配选项 TTS）：`{{1}}` `has`（✅ 不定代词 `Everyone` 配三单）/ `have` / `is` / `was`；`{{2}}` `written`（✅ 双 `t` V3）/ `wrote` / `writen` / `write`；`{{3}}` `uploaded`（✅ 规则 V3）/ `upload` / `uploading` / `uploads`；`{{4}}` `haven't`（✅ 复数否定）/ `hasn't` / `don't have` / `didn't`；`{{5}}` `gone`（✅ 人去打印店未回）/ `been` / `went` / `go`。
   - **复盘语音 `passage_tts`**：`{"voice": "tide", "text": "Our design team is getting ready for tomorrow's client presentation. Everyone has worked hard this week. 6. **`reading_comprehension` #1（L3：差旅群备忘录阅读——辨识 `'s = has` 与 `been to / gone to`）**
   - **材料草案**（约 96 词；标题 + 1–2 句拆 `tts` 切片，配等长 `study_translation.material`；全口语词避免冒号时刻与全大写缩写）：
     - 标题：`Team Check-In Before the Shanghai Trip`
     - 段 1：`Hi everyone, here is a quick update before our Shanghai trip. Anna has booked three hotel rooms near the conference center, and she has sent the confirmation emails to all of us. David has written the meeting agenda and shared it in our group folder.`
     - 段 2：`I haven't bought the train tickets because I'm waiting for Ben's passport number. Where is Ben right now? He's gone to the downtown clinic for a dental checkup and will return around three. By the way, Anna has been to Shanghai twice, and she says the neighborhood near the conference center is very quiet.`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（英文小题与选项配 `tts` 及 `study_translation`）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `What has David completed for the trip?` → ✅ `He has written and shared the meeting agenda.`
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `Where is Ben right now?` → ✅ `He is at a downtown clinic for a dental checkup.`（考 `He's gone to` 未返状态）
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Anna is in Shanghai right now.` → `false`（精准拦截把 `has been to` 误解为人在当地的 E3 偏误）。

7. **`listening` #1（L3：办公室午间对话，v2 `dialogue` 模式——实战听辨 `'ve / 's`、`gone / been` 与 `have had`）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella" }`；父级 `stem` 禁配 TTS；6 行 `transcript` 对齐 6 条 `study_translation.material`：
     1. `W` (Grace): `"Hi, Kevin! Have you finished the monthly budget sheet?"`
     2. `M` (Kevin): `"Yes, I have. I've checked all the numbers and sent the file to Sara."`
     3. `W` (Grace): `"Great! Speaking of Sara, is she at her desk? I need her signature on this form."`

   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（配 `tts` 与译文）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `What has Kevin done with the monthly budget sheet?` → ✅ `He has checked the numbers and sent it to Sara.`
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `Why can't Grace get Sara's signature right now?` → ✅ `Because Sara has gone to the post office.`
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Grace is still at the new noodle café right now.` → `false`（核心检测 `I've been to` 已归来语义）。

8. **`translation` #1（L3 中译英 `zh_to_en`：综合打击 E1 / E2）**
   - **`source_text`**：`"我的经理写完了报告并把文件发给了客户，但我们还没订去北京的火车票。"`

   - **`acceptable_alternates`**：`"My manager has finished the report and sent the file to the client, but we have not booked the train tickets to Beijing."` / `"My manager's written the report and emailed the file to the client, but we haven't booked the train tickets to Beijing."`

9. **`translation` #2（L3 英译中 `en_to_zh`：精准转换 `has been to` vs. `'s gone to`）**

10. **`writing` #1（L4 造句 `subtype: "sentence_making"`：完成进展 + `been / gone` 闭环输出）**
    - **任务要求（`requirements`）**：请用 2–3 句完整英文（20–45 词）向同事或朋友同步近期进展：① 用 `have / has + 不规则动词过去分词` 写出 1 项已完成的事情；② 用 `haven't / hasn't + 过去分词` 写出 1 项尚未完成的事情；③ 正确使用 1 次 `have/has been to` 或 `have/has gone to`。注意主谓一致与过去分词拼写，不混用过去式。

### 配套口语微纠偏练习（Speaking Micro-Drills）
1. **缩写辅音保底跟读**：对比朗读 `I've **SENT** the file.`（保留轻唇齿音 `/v/`）与 `She's **WRITTEN** the note.`（保留 `/z/`），防口语吞掉助动词导致写时漏词；快答 `"Have you eaten?"` → 清晰重读 `Yes, I have.`（绝不吞成 `*Yes, I've.`）。
2. **A-B-C 不规则三态节拍链**：按 `V1 – V2 – V3` 拍手连读高频错题词（`write–wrote–written` / `see–saw–seen` / `take–took–taken` / `eat–ate–eaten` / `choose–chose–chosen` / `forget–forgot–forgotten`），随即造句 `I've + V3`。
3. **`been to` vs. `gone to` 两秒快切**：听中文场景 2 秒张口——“我去过西安两次”（`I've been to Xi'an twice.`）vs.“丹尼尔去银行了，马上回”（`Daniel has gone to the bank and will be back soon.`）。

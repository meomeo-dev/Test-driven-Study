## 一、中文母语高频负迁移偏误谱系（Error Taxonomy）

汉语没有形态时态屈折，也无强制系动词助动词，表达将来只需在主语后加“打算 / 要 / 明天 + 动词”。这种 L1 迁移与先修 `be + V-ing`、`on/in/at` 规则的过度泛化叠加，在 A2 阶段集中表现为以下 6 类高频错误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移 / 干扰根源 | 典型误句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **漏掉 `be` 动词或主谓不一致** | 汉语“我打算去……/明天见客户”无 `be` 对应词；口语缩写弱读导致漏写 | `*I going to book a hotel.`<br>`*Where you going to stay?`<br>`*Do you going to join us?`<br>`*We meeting the client at 3.` | `I'm going to book a hotel.`<br>`Where are you going to stay?`<br>`Are you going to join us?`<br>`We're meeting the client at 3.` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`going to` 后误加 `-ing`、`-s` 或漏 `to`** | 受 `be going` 进行时拖带加 `-ing`；受三单主语影响加 `-s`；漏写不定式 `to` | `*I'm going to visiting Kyoto.`<br>`*She is going to buys a laptop.`<br>`*We are going have lunch.` | `I'm going to visit Kyoto.`<br>`She is going to buy a laptop.`<br>`We are going to have lunch.` | **P0** |
| **E3** | **`going to go / come` 冗余及 `gonna` 误用** | 逐词直译“打算去机场”；误把 `going to + 地点` 当不定式缩成 `gonna` | `?I'm going to go to the airport.`<br>`?Are you going to come to dinner?`<br>`*I'm gonna the supermarket.` | `I'm going to the airport.`<br>`Are you coming to dinner?`<br>`I'm going to the supermarket.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **现在进行时误用于预测、静态动词或无时间锚点** | 未区分“可人为预约的安排”与“不可控预测/状态”；省略将来时间引发歧义 | `*Look at the clouds. It's raining soon.`<br>`*Next week is being very busy.`<br>`?I'm seeing my dentist.`（表将来缺时间） | `Look at the clouds. It's going to rain soon.`<br>`Next week is going to be very busy.`<br>`I'm seeing my dentist tomorrow at 4:00.` | **P1** |
| **E5** | **将来时间状语误加介词或语序倒置** | 过度泛化 `on Friday / in the morning`；直译“今晚”为 `*this night`；时间抢在地点前 | `*on next Monday` / `*in next week`<br>`*at this weekend` / `*this night`<br>`?We're meeting tomorrow at the café.` | `next Monday` / `next week`<br>`this weekend` / `tonight`<br>`We're meeting at the café tomorrow.` | **P1** |
| **E6** | **一般现在时代替个人计划 / 杂糅 `will`** | 中文无将来形态，直接套一般现在时；或误拼 `will + V-ing / going to` | `*I meet Sarah tonight.`<br>`*She will flying to Chengdu tomorrow.` | `I'm meeting Sarah tonight.`<br>`She's flying to Chengdu tomorrow.` | **P2** |

### 核心偏误深度纠偏要点（针对 P0 三大顽疾）

1. **纠偏 E1（漏 `be` 与助动词混用）**：
   - 强化双轨骨架：`be going to` 与进行时表将来都是 **`be` 动词家族**，变否定直接加 `not`（`isn't / aren't / 'm not`），变疑问把 `Am / Is / Are` 提至句首，**绝不引入 `do / does`**（❌ `*Do you going to attend?` → ✅ `Are you going to attend?`）。
   - 补漏简短回答规则：肯定简答不可缩写（❌ `*Yes, I'm.` → ✅ `Yes, I am.`），否定简答常用缩写（`No, I'm not.` / `No, she isn't.`）。
2. **纠偏 E2（`going to` 后动词乱变形）**：
   - 建立“分工铁律”：**前面的 `be` 负责随主语变身（`am / is / are`），`to` 后面的动作词一律保持裸原形**——既不因 `going` 加 `-ing`，也不因 `he / she / it` 加 `-s` 或过去式 `-ed`。
   - 例句对照：✅ `He is going to **send** the slides tonight.`（❌ `*sending` / `*sends` / `*sent`）。
3. **纠偏 E3（`going to go` 冗余与 `to` 词性分界）**：
   - 依据 *Cambridge Dictionary (English Grammar Today)*，`I'm going to go to Paris next week` 语法可接受但明显冗长；母语者在日常与职场中普遍用现在进行时直接精简为 **`I'm going to Paris next week`**，或改用更精准动词（`fly to / visit / head to`）。
   - 严守口语 `gonna` 边界：仅当 `going to` 后接**动词原形**（表打算/预测）时，非正式口语才可弱读为 `/ˈɡʌnə/`（听力需能听懂，书面一律写 `going to`）；当 `going to` 后接**地点名词**（`to` 为介词）时，绝不能说 `*gonna`（✅ `We're going to the station.`）。

## 二、易混语境辨析矩阵与三步自检法

### 1. 四组核心易混语境对比

| 辨析维度 | 形式 A | 形式 B | 关键判别依据与地道对照 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. 此刻正在做 vs. 将来确定日程** | 现在进行时 + 此刻语境（`now / right now`） | 现在进行时 + **将来时间锚点**（`tomorrow / at 6:00`） | 形态完全相同，全靠时间状语定锚。<br>• *I'm having lunch right now.*（此刻在吃）<br>• *I'm having lunch with a client **at 12:30 tomorrow**.*（明午已约好） |
| **2. 个人打算 vs. 已敲定日程** | `be going to + V原形`（Intention） | `be + V-ing`（Fixed Arrangement） | 心里有打算但未定细节 → `be going to`；已约好人、买票、挂号或写入日历 → 首选 `be + V-ing`。<br>• *I'm going to take a yoga class soon.*（打算）<br>• *I'm starting my yoga class **on Tuesday evening**.*（已报名排期） |
| **3. 迹象预测 / 状态 vs. 可控日程** | `be going to + V原形`（**唯一正确**） | `be + V-ing`（❌ 不可用） | 天气、延误、掉落等不可人为预约的事件，以及 `be / need / know` 等静态动词，**只能用 `be going to`**。<br>• *Look at the traffic—we're going to be late.*（❌ `*we're being late`） |
| **4. 打算做某事 vs. 即将去某地** | `be going to + 动词原形` | `be going to + 地点名词` | 前者 `to` 是不定式符号（后接动作），后者 `to` 是方向介词（后接地点，避免 `going to go` 冗余）。<br>• *She's going to **book** a flight.* vs. *She's going to **the airport**.* |

> **语用重叠提示（防机械判错）**：若一件个人计划既已决定又带较明确时间（如 *I'm going to meet David after work* vs. *I'm meeting David after work*），两种表达在真实英语中均成立。命题时应通过清晰上下文线索（如“已预约/已购票/看日历”vs.“只有想法/看眼前乌云或拥堵迹象”）拉开唯一最佳项。

### 2. 学习者“三步排错自检法”
1. **定语境**：眼前迹象预测或接 `be + 形容词` → 必选 `be going to`；已定好时间、地点或约好他人的行程/预约 → 优先 `be + V-ing` 并补将来时间；心里有想法、细节未定 → `be going to`。
2. **查形态**：① `am / is / are` 有没有漏且与主语匹配？② `going to` 后是不是纯动词原形（无 `-ing/-s`）？③ 主干动词是 `go / come` 时是否简化掉了冗余 `going to go`？
3. **查时间与语序**：`next / this / tomorrow / tonight` 前删掉多余 `in / on / at`；句末遵循“**先地点、后时间，小时间在前**”（如 `at the hotel lobby at 9:00 tomorrow`）。

## 三、讲义示范例题（对应 `min_examples: 3`）

### 例题 1：形态排雷改错（专治 E1 / E2 / E3 / E5）
- **题干**：找出下列 4 句日常与职场句子中的错误并改成自然地道的英文：
  1. `Tom going to visits his parents on next Sunday.`
  2. `Are you going to booking a meeting room for tomorrow?`
  3. `We are going to go to the train station in this evening.`
  4. `Do she going to join the online call?`
- **参考解答**：
  1. `Tom is going to visit his parents next Sunday.`（补 `is`；`to` 后用原形 `visit`；`next Sunday` 前去 `on`。）
  2. `Are you going to book a meeting room for tomorrow?`（`going to` 后用动词原形 `book`，不加 `-ing`。）
  3. `We are going to the train station this evening.`（简化 `going to go`；`this evening` 前去 `in`。）
  4. `Is she going to join the online call?`（`be going to` 疑问句提 `Is`，不用 `Do`。）

### 例题 2：语境辨析二选一（`be going to` vs. 现在进行时表安排）
- **题干**：根据括号内语境线索，选择最恰当的表达：
  1. `Look at those dark clouds. It ____ (is raining / is going to rain) soon.`（眼前天气迹象）
  2. `I already booked the clinic. I ____ (am seeing / am going to seeing) the dentist at 3:00 tomorrow.`（已确认预约）
  3. `Our schedule is full—next week ____ (is being / is going to be) very busy.`（静态动词状态预测）
- **参考解答**：`1. is going to rain`（天气迹象预测禁选现在进行时）；`2. am seeing`（已敲定看诊预约，且第二项含 `going to + V-ing` 硬错）；`3. is going to be`（`be` 为静态动词，不用进行时表将来）。

### 例题 3：日常与职场汉译英（整合日程与打算）
- **题干**：将下面两句译为地道英文：
  1. 她下周二上午九点半飞往成都，晚上和客户吃晚饭。
  2. 我这个周末打算整理公寓，不打算加班看邮件。
- **参考解答**：
  1. `She's flying to Chengdu at 9:30 next Tuesday morning, and she's having dinner with a client in the evening.`（已敲定差旅与饭局，用现在进行时；`next Tuesday` 零介词。）
  2. `I'm going to clean my apartment this weekend. I'm not going to check work emails.`（个人打算与否定结构，保持 `going to + 原形`。）

## 四、听说读写四层训练体系与 9 题完整蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

本讲 `budget_plan.json` 核定配额为 **9 题、覆盖 8 种题型**（`single_choice` × 2、`multiple_choice` × 1、`true_false` × 1、`cloze` × 1、`reading_comprehension` × 1、`listening` × 1、`translation` × 1、`writing: sentence_making` × 1；`practice_design_min = 29.3`）。

| 训练层级 | 对应题型与数量 | 聚焦技能 | 核心纠偏与测评目标 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 单点排雷** | `single_choice` × 2、`true_false` × 1 | 语法 + 读 | 精准拦截 E1（漏 `be`）、E2（`-ing/-s`）、E3（`going to go`）、E4（预测禁用进行时） |
| **L2 语篇综合** | `multiple_choice` × 1、`cloze` × 1 | 读 + 语法 | 多句辨错；在连贯日程短文中统筹 `be` 一致、原形、进行时日程与零介词时间状语 |
| **L3 真实输入与转换** | `reading_comprehension` × 1、`listening` × 1、`translation` × 1 | 读 + 听 + 译 | 真实差旅备忘阅读与同事对话听辨（区分已定日程 vs. 个人打算 vs. 迹象预测）；中译英克制母语负迁移 |

### 9 道 v2 题库详细设计（含干扰机制与 TTS / `study_translation` 规范）

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：形态排雷——漏 `be` 与 `going to` 后动词变形）**
   - **题干**（含空位不配顶层 TTS）：选出语法完全正确的一项：`"Maya ____ a new desk chair for her home office this weekend."`
   - **选项**（含病句干扰项，遵循 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组不配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `is going to buy`（主语三单配 `is`，`to` 后用原形 `buy`）
     - ❌ `going to buy`（E1：漏掉 `be` 动词）
     - ❌ `is going to buying`（E2：受进行时拖带误加 `-ing`）
     - ❌ `is going to buys`（E2：受三单主语干扰误加 `-s`）

2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：语境辨析——眼前迹象预测 vs. 不可用进行时）**
   - **题干**：看到窗外乌云密布、风很大，准备出门前提醒同事，哪一句最准确？
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组不配 TTS**；解析内为正确句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `Take an umbrella! It's going to rain soon.`（基于眼前迹象预测，用 `be going to`）
     - ❌ `Take an umbrella! It is raining soon.`（E4：天气不可人为安排，不能用现在进行时表将来预测）
     - ❌ `Take an umbrella! It going to rain soon.`（E1：漏掉 `be` 动词）
     - ❌ `Take an umbrella! It's going to raining soon.`（E2：`to` 后误加 `-ing`）

3. **`true_false` #1（L1：高频规则纠偏，v2 `grading.value: false`）**
   - **题干**（纯中文命题，无 TTS）：判断正误：在英语中，使用 `be going to` 表达第三人称单数的打算时，后面的动词必须加 `-s`（例如 `She is going to books a hotel`）；并且 `next week` 和 `tomorrow evening` 前面必须加上介词 `in` 或 `on`。
   - **判定与解析**：`false`。两处皆错——`be going to` 后永远接动词原形（`She is going to book a hotel.`）；`next / this / tomorrow` 系列时间状语前零介词（`next week`, `tomorrow evening`）。

4. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：多句综合语法与语用诊断，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**：以下哪些关于日常与职场安排的英语句子**语法正确且表达自然**？（多选）
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组不配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `I'm seeing the dentist at 2:30 tomorrow afternoon.`（已定预约，进行时 + 明确时间）
     - ✅ `We're going to the airport by subway tomorrow morning.`（自然精简 `going to go`，零介词）
     - ✅ `Look at the long line—we're going to miss the bus.`（眼前迹象预测，`be going to + 原形`）
     - ❌ `She is going to attending the design review on next Monday.`（双重错误：`to + -ing` + `on next Monday`）
     - ❌ `Next month is being very busy for our team.`（E4：静态动词 `be` 不可用进行时表将来）

5. **`cloze` #1（L2：职场与周末计划短文完形，5 空）**
   - **短文**（约 56 词，原文切片禁挂内联 `tts`）：
     > Next week looks very busy for Leo. On Monday morning, he {{1}} to Nanjing for a two-day client visit. On Tuesday evening, he is {{2}} dinner with his local team at a restaurant near the hotel. After the trip, he is going to {{3}} Friday off and rest at home. "I {{4}} going to check any work emails {{5}} weekend," he says with a smile.
   - **五空设计与诊断点**（全组不配选项 TTS）：
     - `{{1}}`: `is flying`（✅ 已定出差航班）/ `flying`（E1 漏 `be`）/ `is going to flying`（E2 误加 `-ing`）/ `is going to flies`（E2 误加 `-s`）
     - `{{2}}`: `having`（✅ 已定团队晚餐，`be + V-ing`）/ `have` / `has` / `to having`
     - `{{3}}`: `take`（✅ `going to + 原形`）/ `taking` / `takes` / `took`
     - `{{4}}`: `am not`（✅ 否定缺 `be` 排雷）/ `not` / `don't` / `isn't`
     - `{{5}}`: `this`（✅ `this weekend` 零介词）/ `on this` / `at this` / `in this`
   - **复盘语音 `passage_tts`**（仅复盘与背题下发）：`{"voice": "tide", "text": "Next week looks very busy for Leo. On Monday morning, he is flying to Nanjing for a two-day client visit. On Tuesday evening, he is having dinner with his local team at a restaurant near the hotel. After the trip, he is going to take Friday off and rest at home. I am not going to check any work emails this weekend, he says with a smile."}`

6. **`reading_comprehension` #1（L3：邮件/日程备忘阅读——区分已定日程、个人打算与迹象预测）**
   - **材料草案**（标题 + 2 段，约 92 词；`passage` 按标题 + 1–2 句拆 `tts` 切片，配等长 `study_translation.material`）：
     - 标题：`Weekend & Monday Plan`
     - 段 1：`Hi Clara, here is my plan for the next few days. I'm taking the 9:30 a.m. train to Suzhou tomorrow morning and meeting my cousin for lunch at noon. On Sunday afternoon, I'm going to visit a quiet bookstore and read for a few hours, though I haven't picked a time yet.`（注：含 `9:30 a.m.` 的切片需设 `tts.text` 将其改写为 `"nine thirty a.m."`。）
     - 段 2：`On Monday, I'm having a project review with our manager at 10:00 a.m. The weather forecast shows heavy rain on Monday morning, so traffic is going to be slow. I'm going to leave home twenty minutes early.`（注：含 `10:00 a.m.` 的切片需设 `tts.text` 改写为 `"ten a.m."`。）
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（全英文小题与选项均配 `tts` 及 `study_translation`）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `Which plan is already fixed with a specific time?` → ✅ `Taking the train to Suzhou and having lunch with the cousin.`（辨识进行时表确定日程 vs. 未定时间的书店打算）
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `Why is the writer going to leave home early on Monday?` → ✅ `Because heavy rain is going to make traffic slow.`（捕捉基于天气预报迹象的 `is going to be` 预测）
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `The writer has already chosen an exact time to visit the bookstore on Sunday.` → `false`（对应 `going to visit ... haven't picked a time yet`）。

7. **`listening` #1（L3：周五办公茶水间对话，v2 `dialogue` 模式）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella" }`；父级 `stem` 禁配 TTS；6 行 `transcript` 与 6 条 `study_translation.material` 严格一一对应：
     1. `W` (Nina): `"Hi, Ben! Are you doing anything special this weekend?"`
     2. `M` (Ben): `"Well, I'm seeing my dentist at ten tomorrow morning. After that, I'm going to clean my apartment and cook a nice dinner."`
     3. `W` (Nina): `"Sounds relaxing. What about Sunday? Are you going to the park?"`（自然示范省略多余 `go`）
     4. `M` (Ben): `"Probably not. Look at the weather app—it's going to rain all Sunday afternoon."`
     5. `W` (Nina): `"Oh, good to know! I'm meeting my sister at a café downtown instead. By the way, are you flying to Beijing next Tuesday?"`
     6. `M` (Ben): `"Yes, I am. I'm taking the early flight and meeting our design partner at two thirty."`（注：写口语 `"two thirty"` 避免冒号时间读音风险）
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（均配 `tts` 与译文）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `What fixed appointment does Ben have tomorrow morning?` → ✅ `He is seeing his dentist at ten.`
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `Why is Ben probably not going to the park on Sunday?` → ✅ `The forecast says it is going to rain.`
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Ben is flying to Beijing next Tuesday and meeting a design partner in the afternoon.` → `true`。

8. **`translation` #1（L3 中译英 `zh_to_en`：综合打击 E1 / E2 / E4 / E5）**
   - **`source_text`**：`"我明天下午三点要看牙医，而且这个周末打算在家学做新菜。"`
   - **`reference_translation`**：`"I'm seeing the dentist at 3:00 tomorrow afternoon, and I'm going to learn to cook a new dish at home this weekend."`
   - **`acceptable_alternates`**：
     - `"I am seeing my dentist at three tomorrow afternoon, and I am going to learn how to cook new dishes at home this weekend."`
     - `"I'm going to see the dentist at 3:00 tomorrow afternoon, and I'm going to practice cooking at home this weekend."`（兼容确定预约处的自然重叠）
   - **`key_terms`**：`["seeing the dentist", "tomorrow afternoon", "going to", "this weekend"]`
   - **`reference_translation_tts`**（仅复盘与背题下发）：`{"voice": "stella", "text": "I'm seeing the dentist at three tomorrow afternoon, and I'm going to learn to cook a new dish at home this weekend."}`

9. **`writing` #1（L4 造句 `subtype: "sentence_making"`：日程与打算并置输出）**
   - **任务要求（`requirements`）**：请用 2 句完整英文（18–40 词）介绍你接下来的两项安排：① 用**现在进行时**写出 1 项已敲定的近期日程（含具体地点与将来时间，如开会、看牙医、坐高铁或见朋友）；② 用 **`be going to + 动词原形`** 写出 1 项个人打算或基于迹象的预测。注意不漏 `be` 动词、`next / this / tomorrow` 前不加介词。
   - **范文（`sample_response` 单段字符串）与 `sample_response_tts`**：
     - `sample_response`: `"I'm meeting my teammate at the downtown café at 4:00 tomorrow afternoon. This weekend, I'm going to organize my desk and finish reading my English book."`
     - `sample_response_tts`: `{"voice": "stella", "text": "I'm meeting my teammate at the downtown café at four tomorrow afternoon. This weekend, I'm going to organize my desk and finish reading my English book."}`
   - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：
     1. `结构与形态准确（be 动词无遗漏且主谓一致，going to 后接动词原形，无 going to go 冗余）`（40 分）
     2. `语境区分得当（分别正确呈现 1 项确定日程与 1 项个人打算/预测）`（35 分）
     3. `将来时间状语规范（零介词规则准确，先地点后时间，表达自然且字数达标）`（25 分）

### 配套口语微纠偏练习（Speaking Micro-Drills，补足听说读写闭环）
1. **重读动词防漏 `be` 跟读**：跟读 `I'm going to **BOOK** a hotel.` / `She's **MEETING** a client tomorrow.`，保持 `I'm / She's` 快速轻读但**绝不省略辅音 `/m/` `/z/`**，主重音落在实义动词上。
2. **`going to go` 一秒压缩快译**：看到中文“我下周去上海 / 你今晚去健身房吗”，限定 2 秒内直接说出 `I'm going to Shanghai next week.` / `Are you going to the gym tonight?`，切断逐词直译 `going to go`。
3. **听辨 `gonna` 与简答口型训练**：听读对比 `What are you going to do?`（口语常弱读为 `/wɒt ə ju ˈɡʌnə duː/`）与 `Are you going to the station?`（后接地点不缩读）；练答 `Yes, I am.`（清晰发完 `am`，绝不吞成 `*Yes, I'm.`）。

## 一、中文母语（L1）六大高频写作负迁移偏误谱系

汉语缺乏动词时态屈折，常靠句首时间词表达将来，且习惯“主 + 时 + 地 + 动”语序与逗号串句。A2 学习者首次撰写 50–70 词出行计划短文时，失分高度集中在以下 6 类偏误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移根源 | 典型病句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **通篇一般现在时代替将来时** | 汉语凭“这周末/明天”即表将来，动词不变形，导致整篇计划全用动词原形 | `*This weekend I go to Suzhou. We visit a museum and have dinner nearby.` | `This weekend, I am going to Suzhou. We are going to visit a museum and have dinner nearby.` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **中式逐字翻译（动宾与词性错配）** | 直译“坐火车 / 去公园玩 / 住酒店 / 周六有会议 / 坐地铁去”（把 `by + 交通` 当谓语） | `*I sit the train to Hangzhou.` / `*We play in the old town.` / `*We live in a hotel.` / `*Saturday will have a meeting.` / `*We by subway go to the hotel.` | `I'll take the train to Hangzhou.` / `We'll go sightseeing in the old town.` / `We'll stay at a hotel.` / `We will have a meeting on Saturday.` / `We'll go to the hotel by subway.` | **P0** |
| **E3** | **时间与地点状语语序颠倒** | 照搬中文“主 + 时 + 地 + 动”，把时间/地点塞进主谓或动宾之间，或在句尾“先时后地” | `*I on Saturday morning at East Station take the train.` / `*We'll have dinner at 7:00 at a local restaurant.` / `*We'll visit on Sunday the museum.` | `On Saturday morning, I am taking the train from East Station.` / `We'll have dinner at a local restaurant at 7:00.` / `We'll visit the museum on Sunday.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **时间介词误用与冗余** | 受“在周六早上 / 在周末 / 在下周”迁移，错用 `in` 或在 `this / next` 前硬加介词 | `*in Saturday morning` / `*in the weekend` / `*on this weekend` / `*in next Friday` | `on Saturday morning` / `at the weekend`（英）或 `on the weekend`（美） / `this weekend` / `next Friday` | **P1** |
| **E5** | **“一逗到底”黏连句与衔接词错用** | 中文逗号可连接多个独立分句；写衔接词漏 `that`、忘逗号，或出现 `going to go` 冗余 | `*First we check in, then we have lunch, finally we visit the park.` / `*After, we will rest.` / `?We are going to go to the museum.` | `First, we will check in. After that, we are going to have lunch. Finally, we will visit the park.` / `We are going to the museum.` | **P1** |
| **E6** | **漏写 `be` 动词、缺主语或祈使句化** | 汉语“它很开心”无系动词；受中文日程清单影响，省略主语写成无主祈使句流水账 | `*I going to book a hotel.` / `*It will very nice.` / `*First, take the train to Nanjing.` | `I am going to book a hotel.` / `It will be very nice.` / `First, we will take the train to Nanjing.` | **P2** |

## 二、三大核心易混辨析矩阵（写前防踩坑）

### 1. 出行短文“三轨将来 + 时刻表例外”时态辨析（专治 E1 矫枉过正）

学习者纠偏“通篇一般现在时”时，常走入另一极端——把交通时刻表也强行改错。依据 *British Council LearnEnglish (A2 Grammar)*，计划短文时态分工如下：

| 语用场景 | 推荐时态 | 典型触发语境 | 地道示范句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **个人/团队事先计划** | `be going to + V` | 决定好要游览、用餐、入住的打算 | `We are going to visit the art museum on Saturday afternoon.` |
| **已购票/敲定会面日程** | 现在进行时 `be + V-ing` | 已约好时间地点的接站、参访、返程 | `My colleague Lisa is visiting our office next Thursday.` |
| **预期感受、步骤顺接与承诺** | `will / 'll + V` | 结尾期待、行程顺接（`First, we'll...`） | `I think we will have a great time together!` |
| **唯一合法一般现在时** | 一般现在时 | **客观交通时刻表、场馆/酒店营业时间** | `Our high-speed train leaves at 8:30 a.m.` / `The museum opens at 9:30.` |

### 2. 中式逐字翻译（Chinglish）vs. 地道 A2 出行写作替换库（专治 E2）

| 中文意思 | 常见中式错译（❌） | 地道 A2 表达（✅） | 纠偏要点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 坐高铁 / 坐地铁去 | `*sit the train` / `*by subway go to` | `take the high-speed train` / `go to ... by subway` | `take` 作谓语；`by + 交通工具` 作方式状语放句尾 |
| 在酒店住两晚 | `*live in a hotel for two nights` | `stay at/in a hotel for two nights` | 短期出差/旅游用 `stay`，长期定居才用 `live` |
| 去老城区逛逛/玩 | `*play in the old town` | `go sightseeing in / walk around the old town` | `play` 多指儿童玩耍或球类/乐器，不表成年人观光 |
| 吃早/午/晚饭、喝咖啡 | `?eat breakfast / *eat coffee` | `have breakfast / lunch / dinner / coffee` | 用餐与饮品搭配优先用 `have`，地道且不易出错 |
| 接待/欢迎来访同事 | `*welcome to come to our office` | `Welcome to our office!` / `pick up / meet ... at the station` | `welcome` 后直接 `to + 地点`，不接 `to come` |
| 附近有家好餐厅 | `*Nearby has a good restaurant.` / `*There have...` | `There is a good restaurant nearby.` | 某地存在某物必用 `There is/are`，禁写 `*there have` |
| 下周六有个会议 | `*Next Saturday will have a meeting.` | `We will have a team meeting next Saturday.` | `have` 表“有安排”须用人称/团队作主语 |
| 玩得开心 / 期待出行 | `*play very happy` / `*expect the trip` | `have a great time` / `look forward to the trip` | 形容体验用 `have a great/wonderful time`；结尾用固定块 `look forward to + 名词` |

### 3. 英文状语语序（MPT）与时间前置规则（专治 E3 / E4）

依据 *Cambridge Dictionary (English Grammar Today)*，英文中性句末状语严格遵循 **方式（Manner）→ 地点（Place）→ 时间（Time）**，即 **MPT 原则**，且不得插入主语与谓语、动词与直接宾语之间：

| 语序模式 | 句法骨架 | 典型正误对照 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **标准句末序（MPT）** | `主 + 动 (+ 宾) + 方式 + 地点 + 时间` | ❌ `*We'll go on Sunday to the park by subway.`<br>✅ `We'll go to the park by subway on Sunday.`（若带 `to + 地点` 方向补语，常紧跟移动动词后，再接方式与时间，如 `go to the hotel by taxi at 6:00`） |
| **地点 + 时间（写作最高频）** | `主 + 动 (+ 宾) + 地点 + 时间` | ❌ `*We are going to meet at 10:00 at the airport.`<br>✅ `We are going to meet at the airport at 10:00.` |
| **时间前置（梳理行程线）** | `时间状语, 主 + 动 (+ 宾) + 地点` | ❌ `*On Saturday morning, at West Lake we will take photos.`<br>✅ `On Saturday morning, we will take photos at West Lake.`（仅可将时间整体提前并加逗号，地点仍留句尾） |
| **顺序衔接词标点** | `First, / After that, / Finally,` + 完整句 | 句首 `First, / After that, / Finally,` 常规加逗号；`Then` 句首逗号可选（短句常省）；严禁用逗号连接两个无连词的完整主谓句。 |

## 三、写后“四步红笔自检法”与讲义示范例题（`min_examples: 2`）

**A2 短文 30 秒四步红笔法**（可直接收入讲义速查卡）：
1. **查时态**：划出全部谓语——个人计划是否用了 `be going to / be doing / will`？一般现在时是否仅用于车次或开放时间？有无漏 `be`？
2. **查搭配**：筛查并替换 `*sit the train / *live in a hotel / *play in / *there have / *by subway` 作谓语等中式直译。
3. **查语序与介词**：确认句尾“地点在前、时间在后”，检查 `on Saturday morning`、`at/on the weekend` 与 `this/next` 零介词。
4. **查断句与词数**：拆分“一逗到底”黏连句，核对 `First, / Then / After that, / Finally,` 逗号规范及 50–70 词篇幅。

### 例题 1：周末旅行病文诊改（集中纠偏 E1–E6，病文 62 词 → 改写 68 词）
- **原病文（❌）**：
  > *This weekend I go to Suzhou with my friend. I on Saturday morning at South Station sit the high-speed train to Suzhou. First we live in a hotel near the station, then we eat lunch at 12:00 at a small restaurant. In Saturday afternoon, we play in the old town. In Sunday afternoon, we by train go back home. It very fun.*
- **六处病点诊断**：① 通篇一般现在时（`go / live / eat / play`）未标将来；② 语序颠倒（`I on Saturday morning at South Station...` 挤占主谓间；`at 12:00 at a small restaurant` 先时后地）；③ 中式直译（`sit the high-speed train` / `live in a hotel` / `play in the old town` / `by train go back`）；④ 介词错误（`In Saturday afternoon / In Sunday afternoon` 须用 `on`）；⑤ 逗号黏连（`First we live..., then we eat...`）；⑥ 缺谓语（`It very fun` 漏 `will be`）。
- **高分改写（✅，68 词）**：
  > *This weekend, I am going to Suzhou with my friend. Our train leaves at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday. First, we will stay at a hotel near the station. After that, we are going to have lunch at a local restaurant at noon. Then we will go sightseeing in the old town. Finally, we are taking the train home on Sunday afternoon. I think it will be great fun!*

### 例题 2：现代职场接待行程单句改错（3 句精练）
- **题干**：改写下列接待来访同事的 3 个中式病句：
  1. `*Tomorrow morning we at the airport meet our client, then we by taxi go to the office.`
  2. `*Welcome to come to our city! Friday afternoon will have a product demo.`
  3. `*After, we are going to eat dinner at 6:30 p.m. at a quiet restaurant in Friday evening.`
- **参考修正**：
  1. `Tomorrow morning, we are going to meet our client at the airport, and then we will go to the office by taxi.`（时间前置，地点归句尾，补 `and` 与将来时，去 `by taxi` 作谓语）
  2. `Welcome to our city! We will have a product demo on Friday afternoon.`（删冗余 `to come`，补人称主语 `We` 与介词 `on`）
  3. `After that, we are going to have dinner at a quiet restaurant at 6:30 p.m. on Friday evening.`（补 `that`，`eat` 改 `have`，先地点后时间且 `Friday evening` 前用 `on`）

## 四、听说读写练习设计蓝图（严格对齐 `budget_plan.json` 7 题配额）

本讲配额为 **7 题**（`single_choice ×2`、`true_false ×1`、`cloze ×1`、`translation ×1`、`writing: sentence_making ×1`、`writing: short_essay ×1`），下设四阶递进并配套听/读/说微纠偏闭环：

> **v2 命题与 TTS 审计红线**：① 含病句选项的 `single_choice` 遵循 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组禁配 TTS**，仅在 `analysis` 为正确示范句内嵌 `tts`；② `cloze.passage` 禁内联 `tts`、必配 `passage_tts`；③ `translation` 配 `reference_translation_tts`，`writing` 配 `sample_response_tts`（仅批改后与背题可见）；④ 冒号时刻（`9:30 a.m.`、`2:00 p.m.`）在 `tts.text` 改写为自然口语（`nine thirty a.m.`、`two p.m.`）；⑤ `analysis` 禁止出现“选项 a/b/c/d”。

### 1. L1 单点纠偏（3 题：辨语序、清直译、定边界）

1. **`single_choice` #1（状语语序 MPT 与时间介词辨析，含病句全组无 TTS）**
   - **题干**：以下哪句关于周末聚餐计划的英文表达**语序与介词均完全正确**？
   - **选项**：
     - ✅ `We are going to have dinner at a riverside restaurant on Saturday evening.`（先地点后时间，具体星期晚间用 `on`）
     - ❌ `We on Saturday evening at a riverside restaurant are going to have dinner.`（E3：照搬中文把时间地点塞入主谓之间）
     - ❌ `We are going to have on Saturday evening dinner at a riverside restaurant.`（E3：时间状语插在动宾之间）
     - ❌ `We are going to have dinner at a riverside restaurant in Saturday evening.`（E4：具体日期晚间误用 `in`）
2. **`single_choice` #2（中式逐字翻译与将来时综合排错，含病句全组无 TTS）**
   - **题干**：写“接待外地同事来访”的行程计划时，哪一句**语法正确且表达地道**？
   - **选项**：
     - ✅ `First, I am going to meet our colleague at the airport, and then we will take the subway to the hotel.`（时态、衔接、动宾搭配均地道）
     - ❌ `First, I meet our colleague at the airport, then we by subway go to the hotel.`（E1+E2+E5：一般现在时代替计划 + 逗号黏连 + `by subway` 缺谓语）
     - ❌ `First, I am going to meet our colleague at the airport, and then we will sit the subway to the hotel.`（E2：中式直译 `sit the subway`）
     - ❌ `First, we will live in a hotel and play in the city center in the weekend.`（E2+E4：`live in` / `play in` / `in the weekend` 三连错）
3. **`true_false` #1（合法时刻表一般现在时 vs. 个人计划时态边界，纯中文无 TTS）**
   - **题干**：判断正误：写英语周末出行计划时，只要句中已经写了 `this weekend` 或 `tomorrow`，个人打算（如“我们周六去参观博物馆”）就可以通篇用一般现在时；相反，火车出发时刻必须用 `be going to`。
   - **判定与解析**：`false`。个人计划与安排须用 `be going to / be doing / will`；仅客观交通时刻表或场馆开放时间（如 `Our train leaves at 8:30 a.m.`）可自然使用一般现在时。

### 2. L2 语篇与跨语纠偏（2 题：语境完形 + 汉译英）

4. **`cloze` #1（商务出差行程 5 空纠偏完形，配 `passage_tts`，选项全组无 TTS）**
   - **语篇**：*"Next Friday, I am going to {{1}} the high-speed train to Nanjing for a client visit. First, I will {{2}} at a hotel near the office. {{3}}, I am meeting the local team at 2:00 p.m. On Saturday morning, we will {{4}} around the old city. I think it {{5}} a great trip!"*（`passage_tts.text` 中 `2:00 p.m.` 写作 `two p.m.`）
   - **各空正解与干扰项**：
     - `{{1}}` ✅ `take`｜❌ `sit / by / walk`（靶向 E2 交通动宾）
     - `{{2}}` ✅ `stay`｜❌ `live / living / stayed`（靶向 E2 短期住宿 + `will` 后原形）
     - `{{3}}` ✅ `After that`｜❌ `After / Before / Next to`（靶向 E5 顺序短语缺 `that` 与逻辑）
     - `{{4}}` ✅ `go sightseeing`｜❌ `play / see / watching`（靶向 E2 观光直译）
     - `{{5}}` ✅ `will be`｜❌ `is / will / be`（靶向 E1 一般现在时误代 + E6 漏 `be`）
5. **`translation` #1（中译英 `zh_to_en`，配 `reference_translation_tts`）**
   - **原文**：「周六上午，我们打算坐地铁去博物馆，然后中午在附近一家餐厅吃午饭。」
   - **参考译文**：`On Saturday morning, we are going to take the subway to the museum, and then we will have lunch at a nearby restaurant at noon.`（亦接受 `take the underground`、`at a restaurant nearby` 或拆成两句使用 `After that,`；解析重点拆解 `*in Saturday morning`、`*sit the subway` 及“地点在前、时间在后”语序）。

### 3. L3 独立纠错与短文产出（2 题：病句重写 + 50–70 词短文）

6. **`writing`（`sentence_making`）#1（中式病句诊改与重组，配 `sample_response_tts`）**
   - **任务**：请将下面 2 句典型中式病句改写成 2–3 句语法准确、语序地道、含顺序衔接词的英文（22–38 词）：
     - ① `*I on Sunday morning sit the train go to Suzhou.`
     - ② `*First we live in a hotel, then we play in the garden, it very nice.`
   - **参考范文（35 词）**：`On Sunday morning, I am going to take the train to Suzhou. First, we will stay at a hotel, and then we are going to visit the garden. I think it will be very nice.`
   - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：① 将来时与系动词准确无通篇一般现在时（35 分）；② 彻底纠偏 `sit/live/play` 等中式直译（35 分）；③ 状语语序规范、消除逗号黏连且拼写标点无误（30 分）。
7. **`writing`（`short_essay`）#1（50–70 词出行或接待计划短文，配 `sample_response_tts`）**
   - **任务**：从以下场景二选一，写一篇 **50–70 词**计划短文：① 本周末与朋友短途旅行；② 下周接待来访同事的一天安排。须满足：① 交代时间、地点与交通方式；② 正确使用 `be going to / be doing` 与 `will`（不犯通篇一般现在时）；③ 至少使用 3 个顺序衔接词且无“一逗到底”；④ 严守“地点在前、时间在后”或时间前置规则。
   - **参考范文（65 词，职场接待场景）**：`Next Thursday, my colleague Lisa is visiting our office from Shanghai. Her train arrives at 9:30 a.m., so I'm going to meet her at East Station. First, we'll take the subway to her hotel. After that, we're going to have lunch at a nice restaurant nearby. Finally, we'll have a team meeting at the office at 2:00 p.m. I think it'll be a great day!`（`sample_response_tts.text` 将 `9:30 a.m.`、`2:00 p.m.` 转为 `nine thirty a.m.`、`two p.m.`）
   - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：① 要素完整且词数在 50–70 词内（25 分）；② 时态分工准确、无漏 `be` 与通篇一般现在时（25 分）；③ 交通/餐饮/观光或职场搭配地道、无中式直译（25 分）；④ MPT 状语语序、顺序衔接词与断句标点规范（25 分）。

### 4. 配套听、读、说微纠偏训练（补足四技能闭环）
- **听（听辨语序与缩读）**：播放范文慢速/常速音频，边听边勾出每句的 **地点** 与 **时间** 先后次序，并留意 `I'm going to / we'll / it'll` 弱读与清晰辅音收尾。
- **读（60 秒红笔找茬）**：限时 1 分钟通读例题 1 病文并圈出 6 处错误，培养先按“四步红笔法”扫雷再润色的审校习惯。
- **说（先说后写 30 秒口述）**：动笔前不看稿，用 `This weekend... First,... After that,... Finally,... I think it'll be...` 口述 4 句行程并录音回听，重点自查是否误说 `sit the train / play in` 或把时间卡在主谓中间。

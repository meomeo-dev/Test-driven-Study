## 一、中文母语（L1）六大高频负迁移偏误诊断

中文“上/下车”“到达某地”“坐地铁”“走过去”均不区分交通工具空间形态或词性，这是中国学习者在 A2 出行导航中最主要的失分根源。

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移根源 | 典型误句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 测评优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | **上下车介词混用**（`get on/off` vs `get in/out of`） | 中文“上/下车”不分车型；直译“下车”为 `*get down` | `*Get on the taxi.`<br>`*Get off the car.`<br>`*Get down the bus here.` | `Get in the taxi.`<br>`Get out of the car.`<br>`Get off the bus here.` | P0（必考） |
| E2 | **到达动词介词错配**（`arrive in/at` vs `get to / reach`） | 把 `arrive` 当及物动词，或套用 `go to` 写 `*arrive to`；给 `home` 多加介词 | `*We arrived to the station.`<br>`*She arrived in the airport.`<br>`*He arrived at home late.`<br>`*We reached to the hotel.` | `We arrived at the station.`<br>`She arrived at the airport.`<br>`He arrived home late.`<br>`We reached the hotel.` | P0（必考） |
| E3 | **交通方式介词混用**（`by` / `on foot` / `take`） | 直译“用脚走”写 `*by foot`；`by` 后多加冠词；`take` 与 `by` 叠用 | `*I go to work by foot.`<br>`*She goes by a subway.`<br>`*We take by bus to the office.` | `I go to work on foot.`<br>`She goes by subway.`<br>`We take the bus to the office.` | P0（高频） |
| E4 | **指路词性与冠词失误**（`past/passed`、`cross/across`、`left`） | 混淆动词与介词；直译“向左转”加 `to`；漏掉 `on the/your left` 限定词 | `*Go passed the bank.`<br>`*The café is cross the street.`<br>`*Turn to left. It's on left.` | `Go past the bank.`<br>`The café is across the street.`<br>`Turn left. It's on your left.` | P1（高频） |
| E5 | **耗时句型与问路礼貌缺失** | 套用人作主语写 `*It spends 10 minutes`；省去 `Excuse me` 直接盘问 | `*How long do I spend to the office?`<br>`*Where is the subway?`（对陌生人） | `How long does it take to get to the office?`<br>`Excuse me, how can I get to the subway station?` | P1（高频） |
| E6 | **换乘、下车站与站数搭配错误** | 把具体站点用成 `in`；把换乘线用成 `to` 与 `at` 颠倒 | `*Get off in Central Station.`<br>`*Transfer at Line 2 in Park Station.` | `Get off at Central Station.`<br>`Transfer to Line 2 at Park Station.` | P1（听力高频） |

## 二、核心易混搭配辨析矩阵

### 1. 上下车与乘坐状态：`get on/off` vs `get in/out of`（空间判定法）

依据 *Cambridge Dictionary* 语法规范，英语用“**能否在车厢内站立走动（或跨坐其上）**”区分两套介词：

| 维度 | `get on` / `get off`（及静态 `on a/the ...`） | `get in (into)` / `get out of`（及静态 `in a/the ...`） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **物理空间特征** | **大型公共交通**（有走道可站立走动）或**跨坐型工具**（坐于车体之上） | **小型封闭车辆**（需弯腰坐入，无法在车内站立走动） |
| **典型交通工具** | `bus`, `train`, `subway / underground`, `plane`, `ferry / ship`, `bike / bicycle`, `motorbike` | `car`, `taxi / cab`, `van`, `Uber`, `small boat` |
| **地道例句（日常与职场）** | `Get on the Line 2 train and get off at City Library.`<br>`I left my umbrella on the bus this morning.` | `Please get in the taxi; our meeting starts in twenty minutes.`<br>`She paid the driver and got out of the car near the office.` |
| **关键避坑** | 切勿说 `*get down the bus`；下车固定用 `get off` + 车名，或 `get off at` + 站名。 | `get out` 后接具体车辆必须加 **`of`**（`get out of the taxi`，不可省略 `of`）。 |

### 2. “到达”三动词：`arrive in / arrive at` vs `get to` vs `reach`

| 动词搭配 | 适用目的地类型 | 介词规则 | 地道例句 | 高频错句警报 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`arrive in`** | **大地理范围**（国家、城市、城镇、大区域） | 不及物，必加 `in` | `We arrived in Shanghai at noon.` | ❌ `*arrive at Shanghai` / `*arrive to Shanghai` |
| **`arrive at`** | **具体点位/建筑**（车站、机场、酒店、公司、会议点） | 不及物，必加 `at` | `What time did you arrive at the airport?` | ❌ `*arrive in the airport` / `*arrive the station` |
| **`arrive`（零介词）** | 副词 **`home / here / there`**，或单独作结 | **绝不加介词** | `Call me when you arrive home.` / `The train arrived early.` | ❌ `*arrive at home` / `*arrive to here` |
| **`get to`** | **大小地点通用**（口语最高频、最安全） | 接名词加 `to`；接 `home/here/there` 零介词 | `How can I get to the museum?` / `I got home at 7:00.` | ❌ `*get to home` / `*get to there` |
| **`reach`** | 大小地点均可（偏书面或强调历时抵达） | **及物动词，直接接宾语，无介词** | `We reached the hotel before sunset.` | ❌ `*reach to the hotel` / `*reach at the office` |

### 3. 交通方式与指路高频易混组

| 对比组 | 核心规则 | 地道对比例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`by + 零冠词名词`** vs **`on / in + 限定词`** vs **`on foot`** | 泛指交通方式用 `by + 光杆名词`（无 `a/the/my`）；若出现 `a/the/my`，大车改 `on`、小车改 `in`；步行固定用 **`on foot`**。 | `I commute by subway.`（✅）vs `I came on the 8:15 train.`（✅）<br>`She came in her car.`（✅）vs `It's close, so let's go on foot.`（✅） |
| **`go past`** vs **`passed`** | `past` 是**介词**（放在 `go / walk / drive` 后，任何时态拼写不变）；`passed` 是动词 `pass` 的过去式。 | `Go past the post office and turn right.`（祈使句）<br>`We walked past the bank.` = `We passed the bank.`（过去式） |
| **`cross`** vs **`across (from)`** | `cross` 是**动词**，直接接街道/桥；`across` 是**介词/副词**，须配动词（`walk across`）或表“在对面”（`across from` = `opposite`）。 | `Cross the street at the traffic lights.`（✅）<br>`The bookstore is across from the subway exit.`（✅） |
| **`turn left`** vs **`on your / the left`** | 动作转向用 `turn left / right`（不加 `to`）；描述最终位置用 `on your left` 或 `on the left`（不可漏限定词）。 | `Turn left at the corner. The office building is on your right.` |
| **`It takes ...`** vs **`spend`** | 表路程耗时主推 **`It takes (sb) + 时间 + to get to ...`**；主语为人时才用 `spend + 时间 + on / doing`。 | `How long does it take to get to the station? — It takes about 15 minutes by bus.` |

## 三、左右转与路口序号听辨失误剖析（Navigation Listening Traps）

学习者看地图文字往往能做对，但在真实英语问路与地铁广播中频频迷路，核心卡点集中在以下 **4 类声学与认知陷阱**：

1. **序数词近音错听（`first` vs `third`，`second` 误听）**
   - **`first` `/fɜːrst/` vs `third` `/θɜːrd/`**：中文母语者对唇齿音 `/f/` 与咬舌音 `/θ/` 辨识度偏弱，极易把“第三个路口”听成“第一个路口”。
   - **省略结构的瞬时解码**：母语者极常压缩说 **`Take the second left`**（= `Turn left at the second street / turning`），若只抓到 `left` 而漏听前面的序数词 `second`，就会提前在第一个路口左转。
2. **“先越过、再转弯”的顺序干扰（诱饵路口陷阱）**
   - 真实指路常先给参照物再给转弯点：`Go straight, go past the first traffic lights, and turn right at the second one.`（直走，**越过**第一个红绿灯，在第二个红绿灯右转）。学习者常在听到 `first traffic lights` 时就停止追踪，把“经过点（`go past`）”错当“转弯点（`turn at`）”或“终点”。
   - **街区计数对应**：`Walk two blocks and turn left`（走完两个街区，即在第二个十字路口左转），不等同于 `Turn left at the first block`。
3. **左右瞬时混淆与多组数字串位（线路号 / 站数 / 出口号）**
   - **快语流连读**：`turn right at the` 连读近似 `/tɜːrn ˈraɪ-tæt ðə/`，`get off at` 连读近似 `/ɡe-tɒ-fæt/`，`go straight ahead` 连读近似 `straigh-ta-head`；不熟悉连读易把 `right` 漏掉或把 `left / right` 反应慢半拍。
   - **数字扎堆串位**：地铁指引常在两句话内密集出现 3–4 个编号——如 `Take Line 2 for three stops, transfer to Line 4 at Central Station, get off at City Hall, and leave from Exit B.`。学习者若不分类速记，极易把 `Line 2`（线路）、`3 stops`（站数，不含上车站）、`Exit B`（出口）张冠李戴。
4. **“对面 / 紧邻 / 左右侧”空间参照失误**
   - 路人说 `It's on your right, across from the library.` 指的是**沿着你前行方向的右侧、图书馆的对面**；听力中常误将参照地标 `library` 当成目的地本身。

### 学习者四步导航听辨防错法（建议写入讲义卡片）

1. **一锁动作词**：区分**直行/经过**（`go straight / go past`——不停不转）、**转向**（`turn left/right / take the Nth left`）与**落点**（`on your right / across from / get off at`）。
2. **二标序号与数字标签**：草稿纸按 **`L(线) / S(站数) / E(出口) / #路口`** 分类速记，听到 `first / third` 刻意分辨 `/f/` 与 `/θ/`。
3. **三画极简折线箭头**：边听边画 `↑（过银行）→ #2 ←（左转）→ ★右侧`，用视觉替代纯记忆，防止左右反转。
4. **四用礼貌复核兜底**：没听清立刻复述确认——`Sorry, did you say the first left or the third left?` / `So I get off at City Hall, Exit B, right?`

## 四、讲义示范例题设计（供讲义主笔选用，`min_examples >= 3`）

### 例题 1：交通与到达介词纠偏（通勤与出差场景）
- **题目**：找出下面 4 句日常与出差表达中的介词错误并改正：
  1. `It's raining, so let's get on a taxi to the office.`
  2. `Our flight arrives in the airport at 6:30 p.m., and then we will arrive to the hotel.`
  3. `My apartment is very close, so I usually go to work by foot.`
  4. `Take Line 2 and get off in Central Station.`
- **解答与点拨**：
  1. `get in a taxi`（出租车属小型封闭车厢，上车用 `get in`，下车用 `get out of`）。
  2. `arrives at the airport`；`arrive at the hotel`（机场与酒店均为具体点位，一律用 `arrive at`；切忌 `*arrive to`）。
  3. `on foot`（步行固定用 `on foot`，不用 `by foot`）。
  4. `get off at Central Station`（具体站点前用介词 `at`）。

### 例题 2：指路词性排错与礼貌问路改写
- **题目**：改正下列指路句中的语法或语用问题：
  1. `Where is City Library?`（在街头向陌生人问路，请改为礼貌表达）
  2. `Walk straight, go passed the coffee shop, and turn to right.`
  3. `The post office is cross from the bank, on left.`
  4. `How long do I spend to get to the train station?`
- **解答与点拨**：
  1. `Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to City Library?`（加礼貌开场 `Excuse me` 与委婉问句）。
  2. `Walk straight, go past the coffee shop, and turn right.`（`go` 后接介词 `past`；`turn right` 不加 `to`）。
  3. `The post office is across from the bank, on your left.`（或 `on the left`；表“在对面”用介词 `across from`，左右方位不可漏限定词）。
  4. `How long does it take to get to the train station?`（询问路程耗时用 `It takes` 句型）。

### 例题 3：多步路口序号与地铁换乘路线追踪
- **材料**：*“Take Line 1 for two stops and transfer to Line 3 at Park Station. Get off at West Square and use Exit C. Walk straight, go past the first traffic lights, and take the second left. The client's office is on your right, across from the bookstore.”*
- **设问**：① 在哪一站换乘哪条线？② 走出 `Exit C` 后在第几个路口左转？③ 客户办公楼在书店的什么位置？
- **解答**：① 在 `Park Station` 换乘 **`Line 3`**（前段坐 `Line 1` 仅 2 站）；② 在**第二个路口**左转（先 `go past` 越过第一个红绿灯，再 `take the second left`）；③ 位于书店**对面（`across from`）**、行进方向的**右侧（`on your right`）**。

## 五、听说读写四技能导航练习蓝图（严格对齐 `budget_plan.json` 10 题配额）

本讲练习配额共 **10 题**（`listening ×2`、`cloze ×1`、`translation ×2`、`single_choice ×2`、`multiple_choice ×1`、`true_false ×2`），按四层递进覆盖全部 6 类偏误与听辨难点：

| 层级 | 对应题型与数量 | 核心诊断目标 | 技能侧重 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 介词与规则排雷** | `single_choice ×2`、`true_false ×2` | E1（`get on/off` vs `get in/out of`）、E2（`arrive in/at/home`）、E3（`by / on foot`）、E4（`go past` / 路口序号） | 读 + 语法诊断 |
| **L2 语篇综合运用** | `multiple_choice ×1`、`cloze ×1` | 混合辨错 + 赴客户办公室 5 空连贯路线完形 | 读 + 语篇语法 |

> **TTS 与 v2 题库硬规则（严守 `design-course-tts`）**：
> ① 含语法错句干扰项的选择题遵循 `uniformOptionAudio`，**全组选项不配 TTS**，仅在 `analysis` 中为正确句内嵌 `tts`；② 父级 `listening.stem` 纯中文且**零 TTS**；③ `cloze.passage` 严禁内联 `tts`，必配 `passage_tts`；④ `listening` 须配齐行数一致的 `study_translation.material` 与 `study_translation.sub_questions`；⑤ `analysis` 严禁出现“选项 a/b/c/d”。

### 10 道题目详细设计蓝图

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：上下车介词 `get on/off` vs `get in/out of` 与 `by / take`）**
   - **题干**：同事提醒你先坐 12 路公交到中央公园下车，再打出租车去客户公司，以下哪句英文完全正确？
   - **选项**（含病句，全组无 TTS）：
     - `a`: `Take Bus 12, get off at Central Park, and then get in a taxi.`（✅ 大车下车 `get off at` + 小车上车 `get in`）
     - `b`: `Take Bus 12, get out of at Central Park, and then get on a taxi.`（诱饵：大型车与小型车介词完全颠倒）
     - `c`: `Take by Bus 12, get down at Central Park, and then get on a taxi.`（诱饵：`take by` 叠用 + 中式 `*get down` + `*get on a taxi`）
     - `d`: `Go by a bus, get off in Central Park, and then get in a taxi.`（诱饵：`*by a bus` 多余冠词 + 站点误用 `in`）
2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：到达三动词 `arrive in/at/home`、`reach` 与耗时句型）**
   - **题干**：同事明天出差飞往伦敦，以下哪句关于到达与路程耗时的表达完全正确？
   - **选项**（含病句，全组无 TTS）：
     - `a`: `He will arrive in London at noon, and it takes 40 minutes to get to the hotel.`（✅）
     - `b`: `He will arrive at London at noon, and it spends 40 minutes to the hotel.`（诱饵：城市误用 `at` + `*it spends`）
     - `c`: `He will arrive to the airport early and reach to the hotel before dinner.`（诱饵：`*arrive to` + `*reach to`）
     - `d`: `He will arrive at home on Friday evening by a train.`（诱饵：`*arrive at home` + `*by a train`）
3. **`true_false` #1（L1：`get on/off` 空间原则与 `arrive` 零介词排雷）**
   - **题干**：判断正误：在英语中，表示上公交车或地铁用 `get on`，上私家车或出租车用 `get in`；而表示“到家”说 `arrive home`，中间不需要加 `at` 或 `to`。
   - **答案**：`true`（解析内嵌正确示范句 `tts`）。
4. **`true_false` #2（L1：`go past` 与 `take the second left` 路口序号排雷）**
   - **题干**：判断正误：路人告诉你 `Go past the first traffic lights and take the second left.`，意思是在遇到的第一个红绿灯处左转。
   - **答案**：`false`（解析点明 `go past` 是“经过不转”，`take the second left` 是在**第二个**路口左转）。
5. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：交通与指路多句综合辨错，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**：以下哪些问路与通勤英文句子在语法和搭配上**完全正确**？（多选）
   - **选项**（全组无 TTS）：
     - `a`: `Excuse me, how can I get to Central Station from here?`（✅）
     - `b`: `Cross the street and walk past the library; the café is on your right.`（✅）
     - `c`: `My hotel is near the office, so I usually go there on foot.`（✅）
     - `d`: `We arrived in the train station ten minutes early.`（❌ 具体站点应用 `arrive at`）
     - `e`: `Go passed the bank, turn to left, and get off the taxi there.`（❌ 应为 `go past`、`turn left`、`get out of the taxi`）
   - **正确集合**：`["a", "b", "c"]`。
6. **`cloze` #1（L2：赴客户公司路线邮件完形，5 空，约 70 词）**
   - **语篇**：*“Welcome to our office! When you arrive {{1}} the airport, please take Line 2 and {{2}} at City Park Station. Leave from Exit A, {{3}} the street, and go {{4}} the coffee shop. Then take the second right. Our building is {{5}} your left, across from the museum. It takes about five minutes on foot.”*
   - **各空设计**（含错搭选项，全组无 TTS；配完整 `passage_tts`）：
     - `{{1}}`：`at`（✅ 具体点位） / `in` / `to` / `on`
     - `{{2}}`：`get off`（✅ 下地铁） / `get out` / `get down` / `get in`
     - `{{3}}`：`cross`（✅ 动词“穿过”） / `across` / `past` / `through`
     - `{{4}}`：`past`（✅ 介词“经过”） / `passed` / `cross` / `over`
     - `{{5}}`：`on`（✅ `on your left`） / `in` / `at` / `to`
7. **`listening` #1（L3 情景听力一：街头步行问路去咖啡馆与路口序号听辨）**
   - **音频配置**：`dialogue`（`M`: `tide` 问路访客，`W`: `stella` 热心路人），8 轮对话，配齐 8 条 `study_translation.material`；
   - **核心脚本（埋设“越过第一路口 + `second` vs `third` 复核”机制）**：
     1. `M`: `Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to Green Leaf Café?`
     2. `W`: `Sure! Go straight down this street and go past the first traffic lights.`
     3. `M`: `Okay, go past the first traffic lights.`
     4. `W`: `Right. Then take the second left, just after the bank.`
     5. `M`: `Sorry, did you say the second left or the third left?`
     6. `W`: `The second left. Walk about two minutes, and the café is on your right, across from the bookstore.`
     7. `M`: `Got it! How long does it take on foot?`
     8. `W`: `It takes about eight minutes on foot.`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**：
     - `sq-1`（`single_choice` — 路口序号排雷）：`"Where should the man turn left?"` → 正解 `"At the second street after the bank."`；干扰项精准设置 `"At the first traffic lights."`（未听出 `go past`）、`"At the third street on the left."`（`second/third` 混淆）、`"Right in front of the bookstore."`。
     - `sq-2`（`single_choice` — 最终落点方位）：`"Where is Green Leaf Café?"` → 正解 `"On the right, across from the bookstore."`；干扰项互换左右侧或把银行当终点。
     - `sq-3`（`true_false` — 交通方式与耗时）：`"It takes about eight minutes to walk to the café."` → `true`。
8. **`listening` #2（L3 情景听力二：地铁换乘数字分离与打车赴酒店）**
   - **音频配置**：`dialogue`（`W`: `stella` 酒店前台/同事，`M`: `tide` 出差访客），8 轮对话，配齐 8 条 `study_translation.material`；
   - **核心脚本设计（刻意分离 `Line 2` / `three stops` / `Line 4` / `Exit B` 与打车 `get in / get out of`）**：
     - 访客刚 `arrive at the train station`，咨询如何去酒店；前台指路：`Get on Line 2 and ride for three stops. Transfer to Line 4 at Central Station, then get off at River Park and take Exit B.` 访客问如果行李多想打车怎么办；前台答：`You can get in a taxi outside the station. It takes about fifteen minutes, and you can get out of the taxi right in front of our hotel.`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**：
     - `sq-1`（`single_choice` — 线路与换乘站听辨）：`"Where should the man transfer to Line 4?"` → 正解 `"At Central Station."`；干扰项设置 `"At River Park."`（下车站错当换乘站）、`"After four stops on Line 2."`（把线路号 4 混成站数 4）、`"At Exit B."`。
     - `sq-2`（`single_choice` — 出口与站数核对）：`"Which exit should he take at River Park Station?"` → 正解 `"Exit B"`（干扰项 `Exit A / Exit 2 / Exit 4` 专测数字串位）。
     - `sq-3`（`true_false` — 耗时与方式）：`"Taking a taxi from the station to the hotel takes about fifty minutes."` → `false`（顺带复现 `fifteen` vs `fifty` 听辨警戒！）。
9. **`translation` #1（L4 英译中 `en_to_zh`）**
   - **`source_text`**（配 `source_text_tts`，`stella`）：`"Go past the bank and take the second left. The library is on your right, and it takes ten minutes on foot."`
   - **`reference_translation`**：`"经过银行后在第二个路口左转。图书馆就在你的右手边，步行大约需要十分钟。"`
   - **`key_terms`**：`["经过银行", "第二个路口左转", "右手边", "步行十分钟"]`（严防把 `go past` 译成“到银行左转”，或漏译 `second`）。
10. **`translation` #2（L4 中译英 `zh_to_en`）**
    - **`source_text`**：`"打扰一下，我怎么去地铁站？我需要在中央车站下车并换乘 2 号线。"`
    - **`reference_translation`**（配 `reference_translation_tts`，`tide`）：`"Excuse me, how can I get to the subway station? I need to get off at Central Station and transfer to Line 2."`
    - **`acceptable_alternates`**：允许 `could you tell me how to get to...`、`underground station`、`change to Line 2`。
    - **`key_terms`**：`["Excuse me", "get to", "get off at", "transfer to Line 2"]`（精准纠偏 `*arrive to`、`*get off in`、`*get down`）。

### 口语听辨与跟读微训练（支撑 `listening_speaking`）
- **最小对立跟读对**：`first left` ⟷ `third left`；`turn left` ⟷ `turn right`；`get on the bus` ⟷ `get in the taxi`；`arrive in London` ⟷ `arrive at the airport`。
- **高频连读模仿**：`get off at`（`ge-to-fat`）、`get out of`（`ge-tou-tof`）、`straight ahead`（`straigh-ta-head`）、`how long does it take`（`does it` 弱读）。

## 一、中文母语（L1）六大高频负迁移与听辨陷阱诊断

汉语否定词（“没 / 还没”）通常重读前置，且不靠助动词形态区分“已完成 / 持续至今 / 将来待办”；在英语自然语速的进度同步中，A2 学习者集中踩中以下 6 类陷阱：

| 编号 | 陷阱 / 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移与认知根源 | 典型误判或误句（❌） | 正确听辨 / 地道表达（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **已完成与待办项张冠李戴** | 英语 `haven't / hasn't` 与句末 `yet` 弱读；只抓实义动词（`send / book`）就抢选“已完成” | 听到 `"I haven't sent the slides yet"` 错选“已发”；把 `"We'll book it today"` 听成“已预订” | 锁定 `haven't ... yet` / `Not yet` / `will` → 归入**待办（To-do）** | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`for / since` 时长换算失误** | 混淆“时间段 `for`”与“时间起点 `since`”；星期/月份相减首尾多算 1 天；混用 `in`（将来）与 `for`（持续） | 周四听到 `since Monday` 错算成 **4 天**；把 `since 9 a.m.` 当“持续 9 小时”；把 `in two days` 当“已持续两天” | 周一→周四 = **3 天**（`for three days`）；`9 a.m.`→`11 a.m.` = **2 小时**；`in two days` = **两天后** | **P0** |
| **E3** | **进行中误判为已完成** | 把 `working on` 与 `worked on` 听混，或只抓 `finished` 而漏听部分量词 `half of ... so far` | 听到 `"She's finished half of the guide so far"` 误判“整份指南已完成” | 结合 `still + doing`、`working on`、`half ... so far` 判为**进行中** | **P0** |
| **E4** | **转折与自我修正诱饵盲从** | *Cambridge A2 Key* 高频干扰：先提旧计划，再以 `actually / but` 推翻；学习者“听首词即落笔” | 听到 `"I planned to send it this morning, but I'm still waiting for Sara"` 误判“今早已发送” | 遇 `planned to / thought` 保持警觉，以 `but / actually` 后的**最终状态**为准 | **P1** |
| **E5** | **`'s` 歧义与时间/事项串位** | `He's` 可为 `He has` 或 `He is`，须靠动词词尾 `-ed / -ing` 辨别；多事项语段易张冠李戴 | 把 `"He's finishing"` 听成已完成；把 `"bought a sofa last weekend"` 错配成“搬家时间” | 按“**事项—状态—时间**”绑定速记，区分 `-ed`（已完）vs `-ing`（进行） | **P1** |
| **E6** | **产出端时态与副词负迁移** | 复述或同步进度时直译“从周一做到现在”“两天前已经订了”“还没更新已经” | `*I work on it since Monday.`<br>`*We have booked it two days ago.`<br>`*I haven't updated it already.` | `I've worked on it since Monday.`<br>`We booked it two days ago.`<br>`I haven't updated it yet.` | **P2** |

## 二、核心听辨易混辨析矩阵与防错策略

### 1. 四类任务状态听辨信号对照（破解“张冠李戴”）

| 任务状态 | 核心语法形态 | 高频听辨信号词 | 高危错听诱饵 | 地道日常与职场例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **已完成（Done ✓）** | `have/has + done`；一般过去时 | `already`, `just`, `finished`, `all set`, `updated yesterday` | 勿把 `just finished` 与部分完成 `finished half` 混淆 | `I've already sent the invite to all twelve guests.`<br>`Mia updated the mailing list yesterday.` |
| **进行中（Doing …）** | `be + doing`；`have/has + done + so far`（部分量） | `still working on`, `half of ... so far`, `almost done` | 听见 `finished` 漏听 `half / so far`；`working` 听成 `worked` | `She's still working on the cover design.`<br>`So far, we've finished two of the four chapters.` |
| **待办 / 受阻（To-do ✗）** | `haven't/hasn't + done ... yet`；`still waiting for` | `not yet`, `haven't ... yet`, `still waiting for`, `need to` | 漏听否定 `n't` 与句末弱读 `yet`，只听动词原意 | `Have you chosen a hotel yet? Not yet.`<br>`We haven't tested the payment button yet.` |
| **旧计划推翻 / 将来** | `planned to ... but`；`will / be going to + do` | `planned to ... but`, `actually`, `will ... in two hours` | 听到原计划就抢答，漏听 `but / actually` 后的真实进度 | `I planned to send it this morning, but I'll share it in two hours.` |

### 2. `for / since / ago / in / by` 听力时间换算矩阵

| 时间标记 | 核心指向 | 搭配结构 | 听力换算规则与陷阱警报 | 地道例句与等价转述 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`for`** | **持续时长** | `for + 时间段`（`three days / two hours`） | 直接给时长，无需加减；**严防与 `in`（将来）混淆** | `I've lived here for three months.`（已住满 3 个月） |
| **`since`** | **起始时间点** | `since + 时间点`（`Monday / 9 a.m. / March`） | **用“当前参照时间 − 起始时间”**：<br>• 钟点：`9 a.m.` → `11 a.m.` = **2 小时**<br>• 星期：`Monday` → `Thursday` = **3 天**（不重复算首尾）<br>• 月份：`March 1` → `June 1` = **3 个月** | `She's worked on it since Monday.`（周四 = `for three days`）<br>`I've waited since nine a.m.`（现 11 点 = `for two hours`） |
| **`ago`** | **过去动作点** | `时间段 + ago`（只配一般过去时） | 动作发生于过去；若状态延续至今，常与 `for` 同义替换 | `She started two days ago.` → 延续至今即 `for two days` |
| **`in`** | **将来时间后** | `in + 时间段`（常配 `will`） | **表示尚未完成**，不可把 `in two hours` 听成“做了两小时” | `She'll finish the cover in two hours.`（两小时后完成） |
| **`by / until`** | **截止期限** | `by Friday / until tomorrow` | 标示截止点或等待终点，提示事项仍未结项 | `I'll send the final slides by Friday afternoon.` |

### 3. 学习者“三步三栏”听记防错法（讲义速记卡）

1. **听前扫题定靶点**：圈出**人物名**、**状态词**（`finished` / `not finished yet`）与**时间问法**（`How long` / `When`），预判是否要做 `since → for` 减法。
2. **听中速填三栏表**：草稿纸划 **`✓ 已完`｜`… 进行中`｜`✗ 待办`**，每项仅记“名词 + 时间”（如 `✓ articles`、`… cover (since Mon = 3d)`、`✗ send draft (wait Sara, in 2h)`），遇 `but / actually` 立刻划掉旧记录。
3. **听后复核两红线**：① 查有无误把 `not yet / still waiting / planned to` 归为已完成；② 查时间题有无直填起点数字（如 `9`）或首尾多算 1 天。

## 三、讲义示范例题（满足 `min_examples >= 3`）

### 例题 1：任务状态“张冠李戴”排雷（周四晨会四句分拣）
- **题干**：将下面 4 句进度同步归入 **`✓ 已完成`**、**`… 进行中`** 或 **`✗ 待办`**，并标出关键信号词：
  1. `"We've already sent the meeting link to every guest."`
  2. `"I'm still working on the budget chart, and I've finished half of it so far."`
  3. `"I planned to print the handouts this morning, but I haven't printed them yet."`
  4. `"Have you confirmed the lunch order yet? Not yet, we're still waiting for the café's reply."`
- **解答**：① **`✓ 已完成`**（`already sent`）；② **`… 进行中`**（`still working on`、`finished half ... so far`）；③ **`✗ 待办`**（`planned to ... but` + `haven't ... yet`）；④ **`✗ 待办`**（`Not yet`、`still waiting for`）。

### 例题 2：`for / since / in` 听力时间换算实战
- **题干**：假设**现在是周四上午 11 点（6 月 1 日）**，根据下列原话换算持续时长或交付时间：
  1. `"David has been on the client call since nine a.m."` → 通话已持续多久？
  2. `"Sara has worked on the poster since Monday morning."` → 她做了几天？
  3. `"Lily has lived in her new apartment since March first."` → 她住了几个月？
  4. `"We haven't shared the report yet, but we'll send it in two hours."` → 是否完成？何时发送？
- **解答**：① **2 小时**（`11 - 9 = 2`，勿填起点 `9`）；② **3 天**（周一→周四间隔 3 天，勿首尾计成 4 天）；③ **3 个月**（3 月 1 日→6 月 1 日）；④ **未完成，两小时后（下午 1 点）发送**（`in two hours` 表将来）。

### 例题 3：多小题听力诱饵拆解示范（微型晨会同步）
- **材料**（场景：周五下午办公室同步）：
  - `W (Maya)`: `"Hi Ben! Have you updated the event schedule yet?"`
  - `M (Ben)`: `"Yes, I finished it yesterday, and I've already posted it in the team chat. I planned to email the speaker today, but I'm still waiting for her bio. I've waited since Wednesday."`
- **设问与拆解**：① *哪项任务已完成？* → **更新并发布活动日程**（`finished yesterday / already posted`；干扰陷阱是“已发邮件给嘉宾”，被 `planned to ... but` 与 `still waiting` 推翻）。② *截至周五，Ben 等嘉宾简介等了几天？* → **2 天**（周三→周五，`5 - 3 = 2`；拦截“3 天”或“昨天”）。

## 四、多小题听力测评与 10 题四技能蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

| 层级 | 题型与数量 | 聚焦技能 | 核心测评与纠偏目标 |

### 10 道题目详细设计蓝图

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：已完成与待办信号词语法排雷）**
   - **题干**：同事汇报“我已经发了会议议程，但还没订好会议室”，以下哪句英文**完全正确**？
   - **选项**（含病句，遵 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组禁配 TTS**；解析为正确句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `I've already sent the agenda, but I haven't booked the meeting room yet.`
     - ❌ `I've sent the agenda yet, but I haven't booked the meeting room already.`（E1：`already / yet` 极性颠倒）
     - ❌ `I have sent the agenda yesterday, and I still wait for the meeting room.`（E6：完成时混用 `yesterday` + 漏用进行时）
     - ❌ `I booked the meeting room two days ago, so I haven't sent the agenda.`（E1：已完成与待办项颠倒）

2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：`since` → `for` 钟点与星期双重换算）**
   - **题干**：现在是**周四上午 11 点**。同事说：`"I've been on this client call since nine a.m., and our designer has waited for the logo files since Monday."` 哪项换算**完全正确**？
   - **选项**（纯中文，无 TTS；解析内嵌原句 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `客户电话已持续 2 小时，设计师已等标志文件 3 天。`
     - ❌ `客户电话已持续 2 小时，设计师已等标志文件 4 天。`（E2：首尾重复计数多算 1 天）
     - ❌ `客户电话已持续 9 小时，设计师已等标志文件 3 天。`（E2：把起点 `nine a.m.` 误当持续时长）
     - ❌ `客户电话将在 2 小时后开始，设计师周一已收到文件。`（E1/E2：时间方向与状态双错）

3. **`true_false` #1（L1：`haven't ... yet` 与 `in two days` 双陷阱，`grading.value: false`）**
   - **题干**（纯中文，无 TTS）：判断正误：同事在晨会上说 `"I haven't finished the quarterly slides yet, and I will share them in two days."`，说明她已完成幻灯片，且这项工作持续了两天。
   - **解析**：`false`。`haven't finished ... yet` 表**尚未完成**；`in two days` 配 `will` 表**两天后**分享，而非持续时长 `for two days`。

4. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：日常与职场进度同步多句辨错，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**：以下哪些进度同步英文表达**语法正确且自然**？（多选）
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `So far, we've collected thirty responses from the online survey.`
     - ✅ `Has Leo replied to the hotel yet? Not yet, we're still waiting for his email.`
     - ✅ `Maya has worked in the marketing team since March.`
     - ❌ `I work on this travel plan since last Monday, and I haven't finished it already.`（E6/E1：持续至今误用一般现在时 + 否定句误用 `already`）
     - ❌ `We've booked the meeting room two days ago, and we've stayed here for last Friday.`（E2/E6：完成时配 `ago` + `for` 误接时间点）

5. **`cloze` #1（L2：周三欢迎午餐筹备完形，5 空 + `passage_tts`）**
   - **语篇**（约 82 词；`cloze.passage` 禁挂内联 `tts`）：
     > Today is Wednesday, and our team is checking the plan for Friday's welcome lunch. {{1}}, we have booked a quiet table at River Café and sent the invite to all twelve guests. Anna has worked on the welcome cards {{2}} Monday, so she has almost finished them. However, we {{3}} chosen the dessert menu yet because Mia is {{4}} waiting for the bakery's price list. She will confirm the final order {{5}} two hours.
   - **5 空设计**（全组无 TTS）：`{{1}}` `So far`（✅）/ `Not yet` / `Since` / `Ago`；`{{2}}` `since`（✅）/ `for` / `in` / `ago`；`{{3}}` `haven't`（✅）/ `have` / `didn't` / `aren't`；`{{4}}` `still`（✅）/ `already` / `yet` / `ever`；`{{5}}` `in`（✅）/ `for` / `since` / `ago`。
   - **复盘语音 `passage_tts`**（仅背题与交卷后下发）：`{"voice": "stella", "text": "Today is Wednesday, and our team is checking the plan for Friday's welcome lunch. So far, we have booked a quiet table at River Café and sent the invite to all twelve guests. Anna has worked on the welcome cards since Monday, so she has almost finished them. 6. **`reading_comprehension` #1（L3：周四上线进度群消息阅读 + 3 小题）**
   - **语篇**（约 90 词；标题 + 2 段拆 `tts` 切片，对齐 3 条 `study_translation.material`）：
     - 标题：`Thursday Morning Team Check-In Notes`
     - 段 1：`Hi team, here is our Thursday update for the new website launch. Since Monday, Ryan has fixed all six login bugs, and he has already updated the help page. Chloe started writing the user guide two days ago, and she has finished half of it so far.`
     - 段 2：`However, we haven't tested the payment button yet because we are still waiting for the bank's approval. We have waited for their reply since Tuesday morning. We hope it arrives today, and Leo will run the final check tomorrow afternoon.`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（英文题干与选项配 `tts` 及译文）：
     - `sq-1`（`multiple_choice`，4 选 2）：`Which two tasks has the team already finished?` → ✅ `Fixing the six login bugs.` + `Updating the help page.`（诱饵：`Writing the whole user guide.`、`Testing the payment button.`）
     - `sq-2`（`single_choice`）：`By Thursday morning, how long has the team waited for the bank's approval?` → ✅ `For two days.`（周二早→周四早 = 2 天；诱饵：`For three days.` / `For four days.` / `Since last week.`）
     - `sq-3`（`true_false`）：`Chloe has already finished writing the entire user guide.` → `false`（检测 `finished half of it so far`）。

7. **`listening` #1（L3 多小题听力一：周四办公室晨会，v2 `dialogue` 模式，4 小题）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella" }`；父级 `stem` 纯中文且**零 TTS**；8 行 `transcript` 严格对齐 8 条 `study_translation.material`：
     1. `W` (Emma): `"Morning, David! Thanks for joining our Thursday check-in. How is the client newsletter going so far?"`
     2. `M` (David): `"Hi Emma! I've already written all three articles and checked the photos."`
     3. `W` (Emma): `"Great work! Have you sent the final draft to the client yet?"`
     4. `M` (David): `"Not yet. Actually, I planned to send it this morning, but I'm still waiting for Sara's cover design."`
     5. `W` (Emma): `"Oh, I see. How long has Sara worked on that cover?"`
     6. `M` (David): `"She's worked on it since Monday morning. She just messaged me that she'll finish it in two hours."`
     7. `W` (Emma): `"Good to know. What about the mailing list? Have we updated the email addresses?"`
     8. `M` (David): `"Yes, Mia updated the whole list yesterday afternoon, and she's already shared the file in our team folder."`
   - **4 道 `sub_questions`**（英文题干与选项配 `tts` 及译文）：
     - `sq-1`（`multiple_choice`，4 选 2）：`Which two tasks has the team already finished?` → ✅ `Writing the three newsletter articles.` + `Updating the mailing list.`（诱饵：`Sending the final draft to the client.`、`Finishing the cover design.`）
     - `sq-2`（`single_choice`）：`Why hasn't David sent the final draft to the client yet?` → ✅ `He is still waiting for Sara's cover design.`（诱饵：照片未查 / 名单未更 / 需重写文章）
     - `sq-3`（`single_choice`，须根据“周四晨会 + `since Monday morning`”心算 3 天）：`How long has Sara worked on the cover design, and when will she finish it?` → ✅ `She has worked on it for three days and will finish it in two hours.`（诱饵：持续 2 小时周一完成 / 持续 4 天已完成 / 两小时前开始）
     - `sq-4`（`true_false`）：`David sent the final draft to the client this morning.` → `false`（反制 `planned to send it this morning, but...` 转折诱饵）。

8. **`listening` #2（L3 多小题听力二：朋友搬家与旅行近况语音留言，v2 `passage` 模式，4 小题）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "passage", "voice_female": "stella" }`；**省略 `transcript`**；`passage` 拆为 4 段 `ContentSegment[]`（段间保留空格，**省略段内 `tts / tts_text`**），对齐 4 条 `study_translation.material`：
     - 段 1：`"Hey Kevin! Sorry I missed your call. I've lived in my new apartment for three months since March, and I really love this neighborhood." `
     - 段 2：`"So far, I've unpacked all my books and set up my study desk by the window. I also bought a small sofa last weekend." `
     - 段 3：`"However, I haven't put up the curtains yet, and I'm still waiting for the internet service. I've waited at home since nine a.m., and it's already eleven o'clock!" `
     - 段 4：`"By the way, I've already booked my train ticket to Hangzhou for next Friday, but I haven't chosen a hotel yet. Call me back when you're free!"`
   - **4 道 `sub_questions`**：
     - `sq-1`（`single_choice`）：`Which task has Lily not finished in her new apartment yet?` → ✅ `Putting up the curtains.`（诱饵：`Unpacking her books.` / `Setting up her study desk.` / `Buying a small sofa.`）
     - `sq-2`（`numeric`，题干英文配 `tts`，中文单位提示单独放纯文本“填写数字，单位：小时”）：`How many hours has Lily waited at home for the internet service?` → `2`（`11 - 9 = 2`，拦截直填 `9` 或 `11`）
     - `sq-3`（`single_choice`）：`What is the current status of Lily's trip to Hangzhou?` → ✅ `She has booked the train ticket, but she hasn't chosen a hotel yet.`（诱饵：订了酒店没订车票 / 两者全订 / 已在杭州住三个月）
     - `sq-4`（`true_false`）：`Lily moved into her new apartment last weekend.` → `false`（反制时间串位：`last weekend` 是买沙发时间）。

9. **`translation` #1（L4 英译中 `en_to_zh`）**
   - **`source_text`**（配 `source_text_tts`，`tide`）：`"So far, we've finished two chapters, but we haven't checked the slides yet. We've worked on this plan since Monday."`
   - **`reference_translation`**：`"到目前为止，我们已经完成了两章，但还没有核对幻灯片。我们从周一开始就一直在做这个方案。"`
   - **`key_terms`**：`["到目前为止", "已经完成", "还没有", "从周一"]`

10. **`translation` #2（L4 中译英 `zh_to_en`）**
    - **`source_text`**：`"你把会议纪要发给团队了吗？还没有。我从上午九点开始就在整理数据，我会在一小时后发给你。"`
    - **`reference_translation`**（配 `reference_translation_tts`，`stella`）：`"Have you sent the meeting notes to the team yet? Not yet. I've worked on the data since nine a.m., and I'll send them to you in an hour."`
    - **`acceptable_alternates`**：允许 `Have you emailed the meeting notes to the team yet? Not yet. I have worked on the data since 9 a.m., and I will send them to you in one hour.`
    - **`key_terms`**：`["Have you sent", "yet", "Not yet", "since", "in an hour"]`

### 配套口语与笔记写作微训练
- **口语最小对立跟读**：`I've sent the file.` ⟷ `I haven't sent it yet.`；`He's finished the chart.` ⟷ `He's finishing the chart.`；`for two hours` ⟷ `in two hours`。
- **听后 30 秒口头复述与三栏笔记转写（衔接第 15 讲）**：听完 `listening` #1 后仅凭三栏速记，用 3 句口头复述（`already` + `since` + `haven't ... yet / in two hours`），再转写为 35 词群消息。

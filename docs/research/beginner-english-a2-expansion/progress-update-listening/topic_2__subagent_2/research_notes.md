# 第14讲「日常分享与工作进度同步听力」场景语料与英中逐字稿设计笔记

## 1. 语境定位与 A2 语料边界

- **课程定位**：`beginner-english` 第 13 单元「个人经历与进度追踪（`experiences-progress`）」→ 第 14 讲「日常分享与工作进度同步听力（`progress-update-listening`）」，目标级别 **CEFR A2**，`skill_focus: listening_speaking`（预算 50 分钟；配额 `listening: 2`、`reading_comprehension: 1`、`cloze: 1`、`translation: 2`、`single_choice: 2`、`multiple_choice: 1`、`true_false: 1`、`vocab_target: 3`）。
- **体系衔接**：保留既有第 1–10 单元（37.33 小时、41 个知识点）与全部既有 ID，弱化车间背景，按通用 CEFR A1+→A2 体系扩展第 11–16 单元（+23.00 小时、+30 个微知识点，共 60.33 小时）。本讲承接先修 `for-since-duration`、`future-will` 与 `schedule-coordination`。
- **场景与语法边界**：聚焦**① 团队周中进度同步对话**与**② 搬家/旅行近况语音留言独白**两类日常与现代职场场景（不含车间/工位术语）。语料紧扣“已完成 / 进行中 / 尚未完成 / 持续时长 / 下一步”，仅使用已学时态（现在完成时 + `just / already / yet / for / since`、现在进行时、一般过去时、`will / be going to`），**不超前引入**过去进行时、被动语态、`should / might`、`although / however`、条件句或 B1 级现在完成进行时。

## 2. 两类场景高频词块库与进度四态速查

### 2.1 团队周中进度同步对话（Mid-Week Team Check-In）

| 语用功能 | 高频地道英文词块 | 中文释义 | 听辨与搭配要点 | A2 地道例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 开场同步 | `do a quick check-in` / `How's ... going?` | 快速同步进度 / ……进展如何？ | 晨会与周中会常用起手 | *Let's **do a quick check-in**. How's the event going?* |
| 汇总已完成 | `So far, I've already ...` / `I've just ...` | 到目前为止已…… / 刚…… | `so far` 配现在完成时 | ***So far, I've already** confirmed twenty guests.* |
| 汇报进行中 | `I'm still working on ...` / `right now` | 现在还在做…… | `still + be doing` 表未完结 | ***I'm still working on** the product photos right now.* |
| 汇报待办/阻塞 | `haven't ... yet` / `still waiting for ...` | 还没…… / 还在等…… | `waiting for` 提示卡点 | *I **haven't checked** the prices **yet**; I'm **waiting for** Mia.* |
| 持续时长/节点 | `for two weeks` / `since Monday` / `yesterday` | 两周了 / 自周一起 / 昨天 | `for` 接时段、`since` 接起点 | *I've worked on them **since Monday** and emailed you **yesterday**.* |
| 节奏与承诺 | `on track (for Friday)` / `I'll ... before four` | 按计划推进 / 四点前会…… | `on track` 表未延期 | *We're **on track for Friday**, and **I'll** send them before four.* |

### 2.2 搬家/旅行近况语音留言独白（Moving & Travel Voice Message）

| 语用功能 | 高频地道英文词块 | 中文释义 | 听辨与搭配要点 | A2 地道例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 留言开场 | `Hi ..., it's ...` / `leave a quick voice message` / `catch up` | 嗨……，我是…… / 留语音消息 / 叙旧 | 语音自报姓名用 `it's ...` | *Hi Emma, **it's Chloe**! I'm **leaving a voice message** to **catch up**.* |
| 搬家与安顿 | `move into a new apartment` / `unpack the boxes` / `settle in` | 搬进新公寓 / 拆箱 / 安顿下来 | `settle in` 不及物 | *I've **unpacked** the kitchen boxes and I'm slowly **settling in**.* |
| 旅行近况 | `been in ... since` / `staying for five days` | 自……起在某地 / 待五天 | 停留延续用 `have been in` | *I**'ve been in** Hangzhou **since Monday**, **staying for five days**.* |
| 尚未完成项 | `haven't bought ... yet` / `haven't taken a boat ride yet` | 还没买…… / 还没坐船 | 听清 `haven't` 与句末 `yet` | *I **haven't bought** souvenirs for my coworkers **yet**.* |
| 留言收尾 | `call me back` / `send you some photos` / `Talk soon!` | 回电话 / 发照片给你 / 回头聊！ | 亲友语音地道收尾 | ***Call me back** tonight so we can catch up. **Talk soon!*** |

### 2.3 进度四态听辨信号对比表

| 状态分类 | 核心结构 | 高频信号词 | 职场同步示范 | 生活留言示范 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **已完成** | `have/has + (already/just) + V-pp` | `so far`, `already`, `just` | *I've **already** finished the report.* | *I've **just** set up my desk.* |
| **进行中** | `am/is/are + (still) + V-ing` | `right now`, `still`, `working on` | *I'm **still working on** the photos.* | *I'm **still unpacking** my books.* |
| **尚未完成** | `haven't/hasn't + V-pp ... yet` | `not yet`, `haven't ... yet`, `waiting for` | *I **haven't** checked the prices **yet**.* | *I **haven't** bought curtains **yet**.* |
| **时长/过去节点** | `have/has + V-pp + for/since` vs `V-ed` | `for`, `since`, `yesterday` | *We've prepared **for two weeks**.* | *I **moved** in last week; Wi-Fi has worked **since Tuesday**.* |

## 4. v2 听力与 `study_translation` 英中逐字稿设计规范

依据 `design-course-tts`（`references/study-transcripts.md`）与 `golden-course-template` 校验规则：

| 维度 | 团队同步对话（`mode: "dialogue"`） | 近况语音独白（`mode: "passage"`） |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `audio` 声明 | `{"mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella"}` | `{"mode": "passage", "voice_female": "stella"}`（**仅认女声**） |
| 原文字段 | `transcript: [{"speaker": "W"\|"M", "text": "..."}]`（长行可加 `"tts": {"speed": 0.95}`） | `passage: ContentSegment[]`（每段纯 `{"type": "text", "text": "..."}`，段间以 `{"type": "text", "text": " "}` 分隔，**禁配内联 `tts` 或 `tts_text`**，禁配 `transcript`） |

## 5. 讲义与题库命题复用锚点（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

### 5.1 听力（× 2）、阅读（× 1）与完形（× 1）

1. **听力 1（`dialogue`）**：用**录音 1**。3 小题：① Lucas 已完成项（`customer survey report`，区分进行中照片与未核价格）；② 价格未核原因（等 Mia 邮件）；③ 判断：Hannah 已打欢迎牌且赶不上周五（**False**）。

3. **阅读理解（× 1）**：将**录音 2B** 加标题 `Mid-Week Travel Update from Hangzhou` 改为 5 段旅行群聊更新（`study_translation.material` 含标题共 6 条）；考停留时长（`since Monday` / `five days`）、当前动作（写明信片）及两项未完成事项。
4. **完形填空（`cloze` × 1，76 词，6 空）**：
   - **挖空版**：
     > It is Wednesday afternoon, and Mia is sending a quick project update to her manager. "__(1)__ [So] far, our team has __(2)__ [already] finished the new brochure," she writes. "We have worked on the website design __(3)__ [since] Monday, and I am __(4)__ [still] checking the contact page right now. We haven't booked the lunch room __(5)__ [yet], because we are still waiting for the guest count. Don't worry. I __(6)__ [will] confirm the room tomorrow morning, and we are on track for Friday."
   - **完整朗读稿（`passage_tts`，仅复盘与背题可见）**：
     > It is Wednesday afternoon, and Mia is sending a quick project update to her manager. "So far, our team has already finished the new brochure," she writes. "We have worked on the website design since Monday, and I am still checking the contact page right now. We haven't booked the lunch room yet, because we are still waiting for the guest count. Don't worry. I will confirm the room tomorrow morning, and we are on track for Friday."
   - **参考译文**：周三下午，米娅给经理发了条简短的项目更新：“到目前为止，团队已完成新宣传册。我们自周一起一直在做网站设计，我现在还在核对联系页面。因为还在等来宾人数，我们还没订午餐会议室。别担心，我明早确认房间，周五进度一切按计划推进。”
   - **6 空考点**：(1) `So`（`so far`）；(2) `already`（已完成）；(3) `since`（接起点）；(4) `still`（进行中）；(5) `yet`（否定句末）；(6) `will`（承诺）。

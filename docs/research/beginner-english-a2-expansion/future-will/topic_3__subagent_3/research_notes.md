## 一、中文母语高频负迁移偏误谱系（Error Taxonomy）

汉语无形态时态屈折，也不区分“事先计划”与“当下决定”，常以“会 / 要 / 将”一词通吃；叠加刚学完第 7 讲 `be going to` 的结构拖带，A2 学习者集中出现以下 6 类偏误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移 / 干扰根源 | 典型误句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **`will` 后误加 `to`、变形、漏 `be` 或混用 `do`** | 受 `going to / want to` 拖带加 `to`；受三单主语加 `-s`；形容词前直译漏 `be`；误用 `don't / Do you` | `*I will to send the file.` / `*She will arrives at 6.` / `*It will sunny.` / `*I don't will go.` / `*Do you will join us?` | `I'll send the file.` / `She will arrive at 6.` / `It will be sunny.` / `I won't go.` / `Will you join us?` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`won't` 拼写错误与 `want` 发音/语义混淆** | 误将 `will not` 拼成 `*willn't` 或漏撇号 `*wont`；汉语缺 `/oʊ~əʊ/` 与 `/ɑː~ɒ/` 对立，听读混成 `want` | `*I willn't forget.` / `*I wont be late.` / `*I want be late.` / 听读混淆 `I won't go` ↔ `I want to go` | `I won't forget.` / `I won't be late.` / 对立：`I won't go.`（不会去）vs. `I want to go.`（想去） | **P0** |
| **E3** | **已定计划、日程或眼前迹象中滥用 `will`** | 汉语“明天要去 / 看乌云要下雨”皆可用“要/会”；把 `will` 当作唯一“通用将来时” | `?I bought the ticket yesterday—I will fly to Chengdu tomorrow.` / `?Look at the dark clouds! It will rain soon.` | `I bought the ticket yesterday—I'm flying to Chengdu tomorrow.` / `Look at the dark clouds! It's going to rain soon.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **`Shall we / Shall I` 人称误用或混用 `Will we`** | 将 `shall` 配第二人称提请求；直译“我们见面好吗”成 `?Will we...?`；后误加 `to` | `*Shall you send me the link?` / `?Will we have lunch together?` / `*Shall we to leave now?` | `Could you / Will you send me the link?` / `Shall we have lunch together?` / `Shall we leave now?` | **P1** |
| **E5** | **`I think` 否定不前置与 `probably` 语序错位** | 直译“我觉得不会下雨”成 `?I think it won't rain`；把 `probably` 误置 `won't` 后 | `?I think it won't rain tomorrow.` / `*She won't probably come.` | `I don't think it will rain tomorrow.` / `She probably won't come.`（肯定：`She'll probably come.`） | **P1** |
| **E6** | **肯定简答误缩写与误解 `won't` 表“打不开/拒绝”** | 将陈述句缩写 `I'll` 搬进简答；不知 `won't` 可指机器或门窗“不启动/打不开” | `*Yes, I'll.` / 误把 `The printer won't turn on.` 译成“打印机将来不开机” | `Yes, I will.` / `No, I won't.` / `The printer won't turn on—I'll call IT.`（打印机开不了机） | **P2** |

### 核心偏误深度纠偏要点（直击 P0 三大顽疾）

1. **纠偏 E1（借力先修 `can`，固化情态助动词四铁律）**：衔接第 7 单元情态动词 `can`——`will` 后永远接**裸动词原形**（删 `to / -s / -ing / -ed`）；全人称同形；接形容词或地点状态须补**系动词 `be`**（`will be busy`）；否定用 `will not / won't`、疑问提 `Will`，绝不引入 `do / does`。依 *Cambridge Dictionary — English Grammar Today*，肯定简答**禁缩写**（❌ `*Yes, I'll.` → ✅ `Yes, I will.`），否定简答常用 `No, I won't.`。
2. **纠偏 E2（“音 + 形 + 句法槽”三维拆解 `won't` vs. `want`）**：
   - **拼写唯一**：`will not` 唯一标准缩写是 **`won't`**（带撇号，禁 `*willn't / *wont`）。
   - **发音口型**（依 *Cambridge Dictionary*）：`won't` 发双元音——美 `/woʊnt/`、英 `/wəʊnt/`（圆唇收口滑向 `u`，押韵 `no / go`）；`want` 发开口单元音——美 `/wɑːnt/`、英 `/wɒnt/`（张口放平，近似 `hot`）。
   - **句法双保险**：听力中元音快读时看后接词——`won't` 直连裸原形（`I won't go.`），`want` 后接 `to + 原形` 或名词（`I want to go.` / `I want coffee.`）。
3. **纠偏 E3（切开“说话前”与“说话当下”）**：依 *British Council LearnEnglish (A2 Grammar)* 与 *Cambridge Grammar*，说话前已决定、已购票预约的日程用 `be going to` 或现在进行时，眼前可见迹象预测用 `be going to`；`will / 'll` 专责**说话当下的即时决定、主动帮忙、承诺**及基于个人观点的预测（`I think / probably`）。

## 二、易混语境辨析矩阵与三步自检法

### 1. 四组核心易混辨析（衔接第 7 讲 `future-going-to`）

| 辨析维度 | `will / 'll / won't` | `be going to` / 现在进行时 | 判别依据与地道对照 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. 决策时机** | **说话当下**临时决定 | 事先打算（`be going to`）；已约好/购票日程（`be + V-ing`） | 临场选点用 `'ll`（`"Coffee or tea?" — "I'll have tea, please."`）；已约日程不用 `will`（`I'm meeting Anna at 3:00 tomorrow.`） |
| **2. 预测依据** | 个人看法、推测（常配 `I think / probably`） | **眼前可见迹象**（仅用 `be going to`；进行时禁用于预测） | 凭想法判断用 `will`（`I think you'll enjoy the museum.`）；看眼前证据用 `be going to`（`Look at the dark clouds—it's going to rain.`） |
| **3. 主动帮忙与承诺** | `I'll + 原形`（效劳/承诺）；`I won't + 原形`（保证不做） | 不用于当场主动效劳或郑重承诺 | 见对方提重物立刻响应必用 `'ll`（`That bag looks heavy—I'll carry it for you.`）；承诺用 `won't`（`I won't tell anyone.`） |
| **4. 提议与请求** | `Shall I ...?`（主动帮忙）；`Shall we ...?`（共同提议） | `Will you / Could you ...?`（请对方帮忙） | `Shall` 疑问只配第一人称 `I / we`（`Shall I open the window?`）；请求对方用 `Could you / Will you`，禁 `*Shall you...?` |

> **语用重叠提示**：不带明显眼前迹象的中性预测（如 `I think it will rain tomorrow.` 与 `I think it's going to rain tomorrow.`）在真实英语中可兼容；命题须给出清晰触发线索（刚收到消息/主动帮忙 vs. 昨天已买票/抬头看乌云）确保唯一最佳项。

### 2. 学习者“三步纠偏自检法”
1. **判语境**：刚听消息当场决定、主动帮忙、作承诺或个人推测 → `will / 'll / won't`；提议同行或问“我来…好吗” → `Shall we / Shall I`；已订票约人或有眼前迹象 → 回到进行时 / `be going to`。
2. **查形态**：`will / won't / shall` 后删净 `to / -s / -ing`；形容词前补系动词 `be`；疑问不用 `do / does`；肯定简答写全 `Yes, I will.`。
3. **查音形与语序**：拼准 `won't`（对比 `want to`）；否定预测优先 `I don't think ... will`；`probably` 放 `will` 后、`won't` 前。

## 三、讲义示范例题（对应 `min_examples: 3`）

### 例题 1：形态排雷与 `won't / want` 改错（专治 E1 / E2 / E4 / E6）
- **题干**：找出下列 4 组日常与职场句子中的错误并改成自然英文：
  1. `I will to send you the updated schedule in five minutes.`
  2. `She willn't joins the online call because she is flying to Xi'an.`
  3. `Will we order some coffee? — Yes, we'll.`
  4. `Don't worry, I want be late for dinner.`
- **参考解答**：
  1. `I'll send you the updated schedule in five minutes.`（情态助动词 `will` 后直连原形 `send`，去 `to`。）
  2. `She won't join the online call because she is flying to Xi'an.`（标准缩写为 `won't`，后接原形 `join`。）
  3. `Shall we order some coffee? — Yes, let's.` 或 `Yes, good idea!`（提议用 `Shall we`；另注意回答 `Will` 疑问句时，肯定简答不可缩写为 `*Yes, we'll.`，须说 `Yes, we will.`。）
  4. `Don't worry, I won't be late for dinner.`（误把 `won't` 写成 `want`，且 `be late` 须保系动词 `be`。）

### 例题 2：语境辨析三选一（`will` vs. `be going to` vs. 现在进行时，专治 E3）
- **题干**：根据上下文线索，选出最准确自然的表达：
  1. A: `"Oh no, I left my phone charger at home!"` B: `"No problem, I ____ (will lend / am going to lend / am lending) you mine."`
  2. `"I booked the train tickets yesterday. We ____ (will visit / are visiting / shall visit) my parents in Suzhou tomorrow."`
  3. `"Look at that tall stack of books—it ____ (will fall / is going to fall / falls)!"`
- **参考解答**：`1. will lend`（突发状况当场主动帮忙）；`2. are visiting`（已购票敲定日程，不滥用 `will`）；`3. is going to fall`（眼前可见迹象预测）。

### 例题 3：日常与职场汉译英（承诺、否定前置预测与 `Shall we`）
- **题干**：将下面两句译为地道英文：
  1. 别担心，我不会把文件发错，而且我马上把最终版发给你。
  2. 我觉得明天不会下雨。我们上午九点在公园门口见面好吗？
- **参考解答**：
  1. `Don't worry, I won't send the wrong file, and I'll email you the final version right now.`
  2. `I don't think it will rain tomorrow. Shall we meet at the park gate at 9:00 a.m.?`（否定前置更地道；提议用 `Shall we`。）

## 四、听说读写四层训练体系与 9 题蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

### 9 道 v2 题库详细设计（含干扰机制与 TTS / `study_translation` 规范）

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：形态排雷——`will` 后接裸动词原形）**
   - **题干**（含空位不配顶层 TTS）：选出语法完全正确的一项：`"Don't worry about the report—David ____ it to you before five o'clock."`
   - **选项**（含病句，遵 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组禁配 TTS**；解析为正确句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `will send`（情态助动词 `will` 后直连原形）
     - ❌ `will to send`（E1：误加 `to`）
     - ❌ `will sends`（E1：三单误加 `-s`）
     - ❌ `will sending`（E1：误加 `-ing`）

2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：语境排雷——已购票日程禁滥用 `will`）**
   - **题干**：补全对话，选出最准确自然的表达：`A: "What are your plans for Saturday?" B: "I already bought the tickets yesterday. We ____ to the art museum."`
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**；解析为正确句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `are going`（已购票确定日程用现在进行时，且自然精简 `going to go`）
     - ❌ `will go`（E3：已提前购票的既定行程，不滥用表临场决定的 `will`）
     - ❌ `will to go`（E1：`will` 后误加 `to`）
     - ❌ `shall to go`（E1 + E4：`shall` 后误加 `to` 且不陈述既定行程）

3. **`true_false` #1（L1：`won't` 拼写与肯定简答排雷，v2 `grading.value: false`）**
   - **题干**（纯中文，无 TTS）：判断正误：表达“我不会迟到”时，`will not` 可缩写为 `willn't` 或 `want`；回答 `"Will you join the call?"` 时，肯定简答可缩写为 `"Yes, I'll."`。
   - **判定与解析**：`false`。`will not` 唯一标准缩写是 `won't`（`I won't be late.`），`want` 意为“想要”；肯定简答须写全 `Yes, I will.`。

4. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：多句综合语法诊断，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**：以下哪些日常与职场英语句子**语法正确且表达自然**？（多选）
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `Shall we take the subway to the museum after lunch?`（`Shall we` 提议）
     - ✅ `I don't think it will rain this afternoon.`（否定前置 + 一般预测）
     - ✅ `Don't worry—I won't tell anyone about the surprise party.`（`won't` 承诺）
     - ❌ `She will to email the final slides to our team tonight.`（E1：`will` 后误加 `to`）
     - ❌ `Shall you bring some coffee to the meeting room?`（E4：`Shall` 提请求禁配 `you`）

5. **`cloze` #1（L2：外出见客户突发应对完形，5 空）**
   - **短文**（约 85 词，原文切片禁挂内联 `tts`）：
     > Mia and Leo are meeting a client downtown at two o'clock, so they already prepared their notes this morning. Suddenly, Mia's phone rings—the café is closed for repairs. I {{3}} forget to bring the tablet," Mia replies. "I don't think the subway {{4}} crowded after lunch. {{5}} we leave the office at one fifteen?"
   - **五空诊断点**（全组不配选项 TTS）：`{{1}}` `will`（✅ 当场决定）/ `will to` / `am going` / `want`；`{{2}}` `print`（✅ 裸原形）/ `to print` / `prints` / `printing`；`{{3}}` `won't`（✅ 承诺）/ `willn't` / `want` / `don't will`；`{{4}}` `will be`（✅ 补系动词 `be` + 否定前置）/ `will` / `will to be` / `won't be`；`{{5}}` `Shall`（✅ 提议）/ `Will` / `Do` / `Are`。

6. **`reading_comprehension` #1（L3：差旅群消息阅读——辨识已定日程、当场决定、承诺与预测）**
   - **材料草案**（约 94 词；标题 + 1–2 句拆 `tts` 切片，配等长 `study_translation.material`）：
     - 标题：`Quick Update Before Friday's Trip`
     - 段 1：`Hi team, quick update on our trip to Hangzhou. I'm taking the 8:30 a.m. train tomorrow morning, and I'm having lunch with our design partner at noon.      - 段 2：`Don't worry about the presentation slides—I won't forget to upload the final file tonight. I don't think the train will be delayed, because the weather looks clear. Shall we have a quick ten-minute call at 6:00 p.m. to check everything?`（`6:00 p.m.` 改写为 `"six p.m."`。）

     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `What does the writer promise to do tonight?` → ✅ `Upload the final presentation slides.`
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `The writer thinks the train will probably be late tomorrow morning.` → `false`（考 `I don't think the train will be delayed`）。

7. **`listening` #1（L3：傍晚归来对话，v2 `dialogue` 模式——实战听辨 `won't` vs. `want`）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella" }`；父级 `stem` 禁配 TTS；6 行 `transcript` 对齐 6 条 `study_translation.material`（全口语钟点，无冒号风险）：
     1. `W` (Emma): `"Hey, Mark! Those grocery bags look really heavy. Shall I help you carry one to the elevator?"`
     2. `M` (Mark): `"Oh, thank you so much! I'm having dinner with my cousin at seven, so I bought some fruit on my way back."`
     3. `W` (Emma): `"Nice! Wait—it's starting to rain outside! I don't think you'll want to walk to the subway without an umbrella."`
     4. `M` (Mark): `"Good catch! I left mine at home, so I'll take a taxi instead. Don't worry, I won't be late for dinner."`
     5. `W` (Emma): `"By the way, I want to try that new noodle place tomorrow. Shall we go there for lunch around twelve thirty?"`（并置 `I want to` 对照上句 `I won't`）
     6. `M` (Mark): `"Sounds great! I'll message you at eleven thirty tomorrow morning to confirm."`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（配 `tts` 与译文）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `Why does Mark decide to take a taxi instead of walking?` → ✅ `Because it is starting to rain and he left his umbrella at home.`
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `What does Emma suggest doing tomorrow?` → ✅ `Having lunch at a new noodle place around twelve thirty.`
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Mark says he will probably be late for dinner with his cousin.` → `false`（核心检测 `I won't be late` 听辨）。

8. **`translation` #1（L3 中译英 `zh_to_en`：综合打击 E1 / E2 / E4）**
   - **`source_text`**：`"别担心，我不会忘记发邮件，而且我马上把会议链接发给你。我们六点半见面好吗？"`
   - **`reference_translation`**：`"Don't worry, I won't forget to send the email, and I'll send you the meeting link right now. Shall we meet at 6:30?"`
   - **`acceptable_alternates`**：`"Don't worry, I will not forget to send the email, and I will send you the meeting link right away. Shall we meet at six thirty?"` / `"Don't worry. I won't forget to email you, and I'll share the meeting link right now. Shall we meet at 6:30?"`
   - **`key_terms`**：`["won't forget", "I'll send", "right now", "Shall we meet"]`
   - **`reference_translation_tts`**（仅复盘与背题下发）：`{"voice": "stella", "text": "Don't worry, I won't forget to send the email, and I'll send you the meeting link right now. Shall we meet at six thirty?"}`

9. **`writing` #1（L4 造句 `subtype: "sentence_making"`：承诺 + `Shall we / Shall I` 输出）**
   - **任务要求（`requirements`）**：用 2 句完整英文（18–40 词）回复一位即将来访的朋友或同事：① 用 `I'll / I won't` 写 1 项当场决定或承诺；② 用 `Shall we` 或 `Shall I` 提 1 项会面建议或主动帮忙。注意后接动词原形（禁加 `to`），拼准 `won't`。
   - **范文与 `sample_response_tts`**：`sample_response`: `"Don't worry, I'll send you the café address right now, and I won't be late. Shall we meet near the subway exit at 6:30?"`；`sample_response_tts`: `{"voice": "tide", "text": "Don't worry, I'll send you the café address right now, and I won't be late.    - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：① `情态助动词形态与拼写准确（后直连原形、无 will to、形容词前补 be、won't 拼写正确）`（40 分）；② `语用功能到位（完整呈现当场决定/承诺与 Shall we / Shall I 建议或帮忙）`（35 分）；③ `语境自然与篇幅达标（贴合日常/职场沟通，标点规范，字数达标）`（25 分）。

### 配套口语微纠偏练习（Speaking Micro-Drills）
1. **`won't` vs. `want` 最小对立跟读**：交替朗读 `I won't go.`（美 `/woʊnt/`、英 `/wəʊnt/`）⟷ `I want to go.`（美 `/wɑːnt/`、英 `/wɒnt/`）与 `She won't stay.` ⟷ `She wants to stay.`；`won't` 圆唇收口、`want` 张口放平，录回放自检元音对比。
2. **`'ll` 轻读保 `/l/` 与简答训练**：跟读 `I'll send it.` / `We'll check now.` 保留舌尖暗 `/l/`；快答 `"Will you join us?"` → 清晰重读 `Yes, I will.` / `No, I won't.`。
3. **两秒语境快切**：看中文提示 2 秒张口区分——“手机响了我来接”（`I'll get it!`）vs.“我明天坐九点高铁去杭州”（`I'm taking the nine o'clock train to Hangzhou tomorrow.`）。

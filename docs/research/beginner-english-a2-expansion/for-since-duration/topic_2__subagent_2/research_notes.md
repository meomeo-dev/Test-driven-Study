## 2. 核心场景词块库与地道例句

### 2.1 现代职场履历与资历介绍（Workplace & Career Background）

| 交际功能 | 高频词块 | 中文释义 | 搭配与时态提示 | A2 地道例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 行业/部门 | `work in marketing / finance / design / sales` | 在市场/财务/设计/销售领域工作 | `work in + 领域/部门`；`marketing` 不可数 | *I**'ve worked in marketing since** 2023.* |
| 就职公司 | `work for a software company / a travel agency` | 在软件公司/旅行社工作 | `work for + 雇主/机构`；延续至今用完成时 | *He**'s worked for** this company **for** four years.* |
| 岗位身份 | `work as a designer / be on this team` | 任设计师 / 在本团队 | `work as + 职位`；持续状态用 `have been` | *She**'s worked as** a designer **since** 2022.* |
| 追问入职 | `When did you join the company / start this job?` | 何时入职/开始这份工作？ | `join / start` 表过去瞬间，用一般过去时 | ***When did** you **join** our team? — I **joined** last Monday.* |
| 旧履历回溯 | `before that` / `worked in customer service for two years` | 在那之前 / 做过两年客服 | **已结束的过去时长**：`过去式 + for + 时间段` | *Before that, I **worked** in customer service **for** two years.* |

### 2.2 居住、相识与生活持续状态 + 细节追问（Everyday Life & Follow-ups）

| 交际功能 | 高频词块 | 中文释义 | 搭配与时态提示 | A2 地道例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 居住时长 | `live here / live in this city / live in this building` | 住在这里/本市/这栋楼 | 延续至今用 `have/has lived + for/since` | *I**'ve lived here for** five years.* |
| 追问搬迁 | `When did you move here?` / `Why did you move?` | 何时搬来的？/ 为何搬家？ | `move` 为瞬间动作，切回一般过去时 | ***When did** you **move** here? — I **moved** here five years **ago**.* |
| 相识时长 | `know each other` / `be close friends` | 彼此认识 / 挚友 | `know–knew–known` 表状态；初遇用 `met` | *I**'ve known** Maya **since** university. We **met** in a French class.* |
| 拥有与学习 | `have a dog / have this bike` / `study Spanish` | 养狗 / 拥有单车 / 学西语 | `have–had–had`；起始动作 `start` 用过去式 | *I**'ve had** my dog Milo **since** 2022.* / *I**'ve studied** Spanish **for** eight months.* |
| 追问接力 | `How long have you ...?` → `Where did you ... before that?` | 多久了？→ 之前在哪……？ | 先问当下时长，再切过去时问背景 | ***How long have** you **lived** here? — **For** five years. — **Where did** you **live** before that?* |

## 3. 时态切换逻辑（现在完成时 → 一般过去时）与口语速查

### 3.1 真实对话“先时长、后细节”对照矩阵

经 *Cambridge Dictionary (English Grammar Today)* 与 *British Council LearnEnglish (A2)* 核对，日常自我介绍普遍遵循**“现在完成时锚定持续现状 → 一般过去时追问过去节点”**的话轮转换：

| 阶段 | 目标时态 | 核心提问 / 标志词 | 典型地道表达 | 语义焦点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. 问时长** | 现在完成时 | `How long have/has ...?` | *How long **have you lived** here?* | 延续至今的时间跨度 |
| **2. 答时段** | 现在完成时 | `for + 时间段`（`five years / eight months`） | *I**'ve lived** here **for** five years.* | 仍住此地，重时长总量 |
| **3. 答起点** | 现在完成时 | `since + 时间点 / 过去时从句` | *I**'ve worked** in marketing **since** 2023.* | 仍在职，锚定起始刻度 |
| **4. 追问细节** | 一般过去时 | `When / Where / Why / What did ...?` | ***When did** you **move** here? **What did** you **do** before that?* | 聚焦**已结束的过去瞬间** |
| **5. 答旧事** | 一般过去时 | `... ago` / `last Monday` / `for + 已结束时段` | *I **moved** here five years **ago**. Before that, I **worked** in sales **for** two years.* | 事件或旧工作已结束 |

### 3.2 四条口语与交际要点

1. **状态动词 vs 瞬间动词分工**：持续至今用 `live / work / know / have / be / study` 配现在完成时；追问起始瞬间用 `move / join / start / meet / buy` 配一般过去时（不说 ~~I've moved here for five years.~~，应作 *I've lived here for five years. I moved here five years ago.*）。
2. **`for + 时间段` 配双时态语义相反**：*I**'ve worked** in marketing **for** two years.*（**仍在职**）vs *I **worked** in customer service **for** two years.*（**已离职**）。
3. **`since` 可接时间点或一般过去时从句**：主句用现在完成时，从句交待起点用一般过去时，如 *I**'ve known** her **since we met** at college.*。
4. **自然口语短答**：答 `How long ...?` 常直接答 **`For five years.`** / **`Since 2023.`**；答 `When did ...?` 常答 **`Five years ago.`** / **`Last Monday.`**。

### 对话 1：茶水间新同事履历破冰（Office Coffee Break: New Colleague Chat）

- **角色**：`Hannah`（`stella`，市场部新同事）/ `Marcus`（`tide`，产品组老同事）
- **逻辑核验**：Hannah 上周一入职，从 `2023` 年起在市场部工作，此前做过两年客服（已结束）；在本市住满五年（五年前大学毕业搬来）。Marcus 在本软件公司工作四年，上月搬到公园附近。

| 轮次 | 英文台词 | 中文对照 | 语法焦点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Marcus:** Hi Hannah! Welcome to the office. When did you join our company? | 马库斯：嗨汉娜！欢迎来到办公室。你是什么时候入职的？ | `When did you join` 过去节点 |
| 2 | **Hannah:** Thanks, Marcus! I joined last Monday. I'm on the marketing team. | 汉娜：谢谢马库斯！我上周一入职的，在市场部。 | `joined + last Monday` |
| 3 | **Marcus:** Nice! How long have you worked in marketing? | 马库斯：真不错！你在市场营销领域工作多久了？ | `How long have you worked` |
| 4 | **Hannah:** I've worked in marketing since 2023. I really enjoy planning events. | 汉娜：我从 2023 年起就在市场部工作，我很喜欢策划活动。 | 核心范句 `since 2023` |
| 5 | **Marcus:** Cool! What did you do before that? | 马库斯：真酷！在那之前你做什么工作？ | 追问前尘切一般过去时 |
| 6 | **Hannah:** Before that, I worked in customer service for two years. It taught me a lot. | 汉娜：之前我做过两年客服，那段经历让我学到很多。 | **过去式 + `for`（已结束）** |
| 7 | **Marcus:** That's great experience. By the way, how long have you lived in this city? | 马库斯：很棒的经历。顺便问下，你在这座城市住了多久？ | 转问居住时长 |

### 5.2 阅读理解（`reading_comprehension` × 1）内网新人介绍（109 词）

  - 欢迎大卫·陈加入设计团队！他从 2022 年起担任平面设计师，在本市已住了四年。

### 5.3 完形填空（`cloze` × 1）短文与朗读稿（58 词，6 空）

- **挖空版**：
  > My name is Nora, and I __(1)__ [have] lived in this city __(2)__ [for] five years. I __(3)__ [moved] here five years __(4)__ [ago] after I finished university. I have worked in marketing __(5)__ [since] 2023, and I really enjoy my team. Before that, I __(6)__ [worked] at a local hotel for two years, so I learned how to talk with many different people.
- **完整朗读稿（`passage_tts`，仅解析与背题可见）**：
  > My name is Nora, and I have lived in this city for five years. I moved here five years ago after I finished university. I have worked in marketing since 2023, and I really enjoy my team. Before that, I worked at a local hotel for two years, so I learned how to talk with many different people.
- **译文与 6 空考点**：我叫诺拉，在本市住了五年，五年前大学毕业后搬来；2023 年起在市场部工作，此前在本地酒店干过两年。考点：(1) `have`（延续至今）；(2) `for`（时间段）；(3) `moved`（搬迁瞬间）；(4) `ago`（过去标志）；(5) `since`（时间起点）；(6) `worked`（已结束旧工作）。

| 备选 3 | `marketing` | `marketing` | `word` | `n` | 市场营销；市场部 | 不可数名词，无复数；表示从事市场工作固定搭配 work in marketing。 | *She's worked in marketing for three years.*（她从事市场工作三年了。） |
| 备选 4 | `for` | `for` | `word` | `prep` | （持续）达……时间 | 后接时间段（for five years）；配现在完成时表延续至今，配一般过去时表已结束时长。 | *I've lived here for five years.*（我在这里住了五年了。） |

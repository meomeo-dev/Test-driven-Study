## 一、中文母语高频负迁移偏误谱系（Error Taxonomy）

汉语表达持续状态与过去事件不靠动词形态变化，一句“我在这住三年了”或“我昨天已经发邮件了”即可直出，缺乏英语对“时间窗口是否关闭”“时长 vs. 起点”与“状态动词 vs. 瞬间动词”的硬切分，致使 A2 学习者集中踩中以下 6 类偏误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移 / 干扰根源 | 典型误句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **现在完成时误配 `yesterday / last year / ago`** | 直译“我昨天已经发邮件了 / 两年前入职了”，将“已经 / 了”与完成时机械绑定 | `*I have emailed the client yesterday.`<br>`*She has joined our team two years ago.`<br>`*We have met last Friday.` | `I emailed the client yesterday.`<br>`She joined our team two years ago.`<br>`We met last Friday.` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`for` 与 `since` 互换及 `*since ... ago` 叠用** | 混淆“时间长度”与“起始时间点”；直译“从两周前开始”拼出 `*since ... ago` | `*I've lived in Suzhou since three years.`<br>`*He has worked here for 2022.`<br>`*We've used this app since two months ago.` | `I've lived in Suzhou for three years.`<br>`He has worked here since 2022.`<br>`We've used this app for two months.`（或 `since March`） | **P0** |
| **E3** | **用一般现在时代替持续至今的状态** | 汉语“我住这三年了 / 我认识她很久了”无动词屈折，直接套一般现在时 | `*I live here for three years.`<br>`*I know Maya since college.`<br>`*Tom works in marketing since 2023.` | `I have lived here for three years.`<br>`I have known Maya since college.`<br>`Tom has worked in marketing since 2023.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **瞬间动词直配 `for / since`** | 汉语可说“电脑买三年了 / 入职两年了”，忽略 `buy / arrive / join` 不可延续 | `*I have bought this laptop for three years.`<br>`*She has joined the studio for two years.` | `I have had this laptop for three years.`（或 `I bought it three years ago.`）<br>`She has worked at the studio for two years.`（或 `She joined it two years ago.`） | **P1** |
| **E5** | **`When` 与 `How long` 疑问句时态错配** | 用 `When` 问过去时间点误用完成时；问至今时长错用 `do / did` | `*When have you moved to Chengdu?`<br>`*How long do you live here?`<br>`*How long did you know Alex?`（仍认识） | `When did you move to Chengdu?`<br>`How long have you lived here?`<br>`How long have you known Alex?` | **P1** |
| **E6** | **见 `for` 盲选完成时、`since` 从句错时态** | 死背“见 `for` 必用完成时”，忽视已结束时长；`since` 从句误用完成时 | 已搬走仍说：`*I have lived in Nanjing for two years before moving here.`<br>`*I've made friends since I have joined the club.` | `I lived in Nanjing for two years before moving here.`<br>`I've made friends since I joined the club.` | **P2** |

### 核心偏误深度纠偏要点（直击 P0 三大顽疾与动词迁移）

1. **纠偏 E1（已结束时间点一票否决完成时）**：依 *Cambridge Dictionary — English Grammar Today* 与 *British Council (A2)*，凡见 `yesterday`、`last week / last year`、`in 2022`、`... ago` 或 `When ...?`，时间窗口已关闭，**锁死一般过去时**；现在完成时绝不与明确已结束的过去时间点共现。
2. **纠偏 E2（`for` 量长度、`since` 钉起点、禁 `*since ... ago`）**：`for` 接**时间段**（`for ten minutes / three years / a long time`）；`since` 接**过去起点**或**一般过去时从句**（`since 9 a.m. / Monday / 2022 / I moved here`）。`ago` 专配一般过去时，不可与 `since` 叠用。
3. **纠偏 E3 & E4（破除 `*I live here for 3 years`，瞬间动作换状态）**：状态始于过去且至今延续，禁用一般现在时，须用 `have/has + V3`。遇瞬间动词，A2 锁定 4 组替换：`buy → have (had)`、`arrive/come → be in / live in`、`join → work at / be in`、`get married → be married`，或改用**一般过去时 + `ago`**。

## 二、易混辨析矩阵与三步自检法

### 1. 四类核心结构全景对照表

| 句式结构 | 现在仍延续？ | 高频触发标志 | 配套疑问句 | 禁用红线 | 地道日常 / 职场例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **现在完成时 + `for`** | **是** | `for two weeks / three years / a long time` | `How long have you ...?` | 禁一般现在时（❌ `*I live ... for`） | `I've lived in this apartment for three years.` |
| **现在完成时 + `since`** | **是** | `since 2022 / last May / Monday / I started` | `How long have you ...?` | 禁接时段或 `ago`（❌ `*since 3 years / *since 2 days ago`） | `She has worked in marketing since last May.` |
| **一般过去时 + 过去点 / `ago`** | **否** | `yesterday / last Friday / in 2021 / two weeks ago` | `When did you ...?` | 禁完成时（❌ `*have met yesterday`） | `We met at a coffee shop two weeks ago.` |
| **一般过去时 + `for`** | **否**（已结束） | `for two years (before / then / in college)` | `How long did you ...?` | 若仍持续，不可误用过去时 | `I lived in Nanjing for two years before moving here.` |

### 2. 四组核心易混辨析

| 易混对比组 | 判别核心 | 地道对照与语境解读 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`for` vs. `since`（含从句）** | **时间段（长度条）vs. 起始点（时间钉）** | `for six months / a few days` vs. `since October / yesterday morning / we met`（`since` 后从句用**一般过去时** `met`，主句用现在完成时 `have stayed in touch`）。 |
| **完成时 vs. 过去时（对话切换）** | **引入持续现状 → 追问过去细节** | `A: How long have you worked here?` → `B: For two years.` → `A: When did you move here?` → `B: I moved here in 2024.`（先完成时定现状，遇 `When` 立刻切过去时）。 |
| **`have lived` vs. `lived` vs. `live`** | **至今仍住 vs. 已搬离 vs. 无时长现状** | `I've lived here for 3 years.`（仍住）；`I lived there for 3 years.`（已搬走）；`I live here.`（纯陈述现居地，**禁接 `for 3 years`**）。 |
| **瞬间动词转持续状态** | **状态 + `for/since` vs. 动作 + `ago`** | `I've had this phone for two years.` = `I bought this phone two years ago.`，阻断 ❌ `*I've bought this phone for two years.`。 |

### 3. 学习者“三步纠偏自检法”
1. **查窗口关没关**：见到 `yesterday / last ... / in + 过去年 / ... ago` 或 `When ...?` → **锁死一般过去时**，删去 `have / has`。
2. **查现在还在不在**：状态至今延续 → **锁死现在完成时**（踢掉一般现在时），核查动词可持续（`live / work / know / have / be`）；若已搬走/离职 → 改**一般过去时 + `for`**。
3. **查长度还是起点**：时间段填 `for`，日历/钟表起点或过去事件从句填 `since`，禁绝 `*since ... ago`。

## 三、讲义示范例题（对应 `min_examples: 3`）

### 例题 1：四大高频偏误诊断与改错（专治 E1 / E2 / E3 / E4）
- **题干**：找出下面 4 个日常与职场句子中的语法错误并改成地道英文：
  1. `I have started this job two years ago.`
  2. `We live in this neighborhood for five years.`
  3. `Lena has known Daniel for 2021.`
  4. `He has bought this bicycle for six months.`
- **参考解答**：
  1. `I started this job two years ago.`（`ago` 表已结束过去时间，须用一般过去时；若强调至今任职则写 `I have had this job for two years.`）
  2. `We have lived in this neighborhood for five years.`（持续至今的状态不可用一般现在时，改现在完成时。）
  3. `Lena has known Daniel since 2021.`（`2021` 是起始时间点，改 `for` 为 `since`。）
  4. `He has had this bicycle for six months.`（或 `He bought this bicycle six months ago.`；`buy` 为瞬间动词，不能直配 `for six months`。）

### 例题 2：`for / since / ago` 与双时态语境填空（聚焦时间窗口与完成/过去切换）
- **题干**：根据上下文语境，填入 `for`、`since`、`ago` 或括号内动词的正确时态形式：
  1. `A: "How long have you worked with Emma?" B: "____ last October. We joined the same project team then."`
  2. `My parents have owned that small bookshop ____ fifteen years, and it is still open today.`
  3. `Ben ____ (live) in Chengdu for two years, and he moved to Suzhou last summer.`
  4. `A: "When ____ you ____ (buy) your headphones?" B: "I bought them six months ____."`
- **参考解答**：`1. Since`（`last October` 为明确起点）；`2. for`（`fifteen years` 为持续至今的时间段）；`3. lived`（`moved last summer` 说明成都居住期已结束，用一般过去时 + `for`）；`4. did ... buy; ago`（`When` 追问具体过去时间点，`ago` 必配一般过去时）。

### 例题 3：个人履历与追问对话汉译英
- **题干**：将下面两组日常与职场表达译为自然地道的英文：
  1. 我在这座城市住了四年了，但我是上个月才入职这家新公司的。
  2. 你认识莉莎多久了？——我们从 2021 年起就认识了，我们是在一次社区跑步活动上遇到的。
- **参考解答**：
  1. `I have lived in this city for four years, but I joined this new company last month.`
  2. `How long have you known Lisa? — We've known each other since 2021. We met at a community running event.`

## 四、听说读写四层训练体系与 9 题蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）

| 训练层级 | 对应题型与数量 | 聚焦技能 | 核心纠偏与测评目标 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 单点排雷** | `single_choice` × 2、`true_false` × 1 | 语法 + 读 | 精准阻断 E3（`*I live here for 3 years`）、E2（`for/since` 互换与 `*since ... ago`）及 E1（完成时误配 `last year / ago / When`） |

### 9 道 v2 题库详细设计（含干扰机制与 TTS / `study_translation` 规范）

1. **`single_choice` #1（L1：拦截一般现在时代替持续状态 E3 + `for/since` 互换 E2）**
   - **题干**（含空位禁配顶层 TTS）：选出语法正确且符合语境的一项：`"Mia ____ in this quiet apartment ____ three years, and she still loves the neighborhood."`
   - **选项**（含病句，遵 `uniformOptionAudio` **全组禁配 TTS**；解析内为完整正确句配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `has lived ... for`（`still loves` 确认延续至今，`three years` 为时间段配 `for`）
     - ❌ `lives ... for`（E3：直译“住了三年”，误用一般现在时）
     - ❌ `has lived ... since`（E2：`since` 后不可接时间段 `three years`）
     - ❌ `lived ... since`（E2 + E6：既错配 `since`，又与现仍居住语境冲突）

2. **`single_choice` #2（L1：拦截完成时误配 `When / ago` E1 + E5）**
   - **题干**：补全职场对话，选出语法完全正确的一项：`A: "Your laptop looks new! When ____ you ____ it?" B: "I ____ it three weeks ago."`
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**；解析为完整问答配 `tts`）：
     - ✅ `did ... buy; bought`（`When` 与 `three weeks ago` 均为已结束过去时间，锁死一般过去时）
     - ❌ `have ... bought; have bought`（E1 + E5：完成时误配 `When` 与 `ago`）
     - ❌ `did ... buy; have bought`（E1：答句 `three weeks ago` 不可用现在完成时）
     - ❌ `have ... bought; bought`（E5：`When` 询问过去具体时刻不可用现在完成时）

3. **`true_false` #1（L1：红线辨析——完成时误配 `last year` 与 `*since ... ago`，v2 `grading.value: false`）**
   - **题干**（纯中文，无 TTS）：判断正误：在英语中，既可以用 `"I have joined this company last year."` 表示“我去年加入这家公司”，也可以用 `"I have worked here since two years ago."` 表示“我在这里工作两年了”。
   - **判定与解析**：`false`。`last year` 为已结束过去时间点，须用一般过去时（`I joined this company last year.`）；表达持续时长应说 `I have worked here for two years.`（或接具体起点 `since 2024`），不可将 `since` 与 `ago` 叠用。

4. **`multiple_choice` #1（L2：跨时态与 `for / since` 综合诊断，5 选 3）**
   - **题干**：以下哪些日常与职场英语句子**语法正确且表达自然**？（多选）
   - **选项**（含病句，**全组禁配 TTS**）：
     - ✅ `I have known Nora since we studied at university.`（主句现在完成时 + `since` 引导一般过去时从句）
     - ✅ `David worked at a coffee shop for a year, and then he started his own online store.`（已结束过去时长用一般过去时 + `for`）
     - ✅ `How long have you had this electric bicycle?`（用状态动词 `have had` 问拥有时长，规避 `*bought` 误用）
     - ❌ `We have visited the art museum yesterday afternoon.`（E1：完成时误配 `yesterday afternoon`）
     - ❌ `Sara works in the product team since last March.`（E3：延续至今的状态误用一般现在时）

5. **`cloze` #1（L2：新同事入职自我介绍短文完形，5 空全覆盖核心纠偏点）**
   - **短文**（约 88 词，原文切片禁挂内联 `tts`）：
     > Hi everyone! I'm Leo, and I'm happy to join the marketing group. I {{1}} in this city {{2}} five years, so I know many great lunch spots nearby. Before this job, I {{3}} at a small travel agency for two years. I {{4}} my current apartment last spring, and my commute takes only twenty minutes by subway. I've used our team's design app {{5}} Monday, and it already feels easy to follow. Please let me know if we can grab coffee this week!
   - **五空诊断点**（全组禁配选项 TTS）：`{{1}}` `have lived`（✅ 延续至今）/ `live`（E3）/ `lived`（与当前仍住冲突）/ `has lived`（主谓不一致）；`{{2}}` `for`（✅ 时间段 `five years`）/ `since`（E2）/ `ago`（E1）/ `in`；`{{3}}` `worked`（✅ `Before this job` 已结束时长配 `for two years`）/ `have worked`（E6）/ `work` / `working`；`{{4}}` `rented`（✅ `last spring` 锁定一般过去时）/ `have rented`（E1）/ `rent` / `rents`；`{{5}}` `since`（✅ 起始点 `Monday`）/ `for`（E2）/ `ago`（E1）/ `from`（中式直译“从周一起”）。
   - **复盘语音 `passage_tts`**（仅复盘与背题下发）：`{"voice": "tide", "text": "Hi everyone! I'm Leo, and I'm happy to join the marketing group. I have lived in this city for five years, so I know many great lunch spots nearby. Before this job, I worked at a small travel agency for two years. 6. **`reading_comprehension` #1（L3：社区语言角新主持人简介卡阅读）**
   - **材料草案**（约 92 词；标题 + 2 段按 1–2 句拆 `tts` 切片，配等长 `study_translation.material`；规避绝对年份过时与冒号时刻风险）：
     - 标题：`Meet Our New Community Host: Hannah Lin`
     - 段 1：`Hannah Lin has lived near Green Lake Park for six years, and she has led our weekend English Club since last September. She loves helping neighbors practice natural conversation in a relaxed café.`
     - 段 2：`Before moving here, Hannah lived in Busan for two years and taught middle school students there. She started her current remote writing job eight months ago. Last Sunday, she organized our first book-swap afternoon, and twenty people joined!`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（英文题干与选项配 `tts` 及译文）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `How long has Hannah lived near Green Lake Park?` → ✅ `For six years.`（干扰项 `For two years.` / `Since last September.` / `For eight months.` 检验多时长定位）
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `When did Hannah start her current remote writing job?` → ✅ `Eight months ago.`（检测 `When` 与一般过去时 + `ago` 对应）
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Hannah still lives in Busan and teaches middle school students there.` → `false`（检测能否读懂 `lived ... for two years` 表已结束经历）。

7. **`listening` #1（L3：咖啡馆偶遇聊换工作与搬家近况对话，v2 `dialogue` 模式）**
   - **音频规范**：`audio: { "mode": "dialogue", "voice_male": "tide", "voice_female": "stella" }`；父级 `stem` 禁配 TTS；6 行 `transcript` 对齐 6 条 `study_translation.material`：
     1. `W` (Rachel): `"Hey, Kevin! How long have you worked at the new bookstore near the station?"`
     2. `M` (Kevin): `"I've worked there since April, so about four months now. I really enjoy the team."`
     3. `W` (Rachel): `"That's great! Wait—didn't you work at a travel office before?"`
     4. `M` (Kevin): `"Yes, I worked there for three years, but I left that job last winter to try something quieter."`
     5. `W` (Rachel): `"Nice move! Have you lived in this neighborhood for a long time too?"`
     6. `M` (Kevin): `"Not really. I moved into a studio flat nearby two weeks ago, and I've already found my favorite coffee shop!"`
   - **3 道 `sub_questions`**（配 `tts` 与译文）：
     - `sq-1` (`single_choice`): `How long has Kevin worked at the new bookstore?` → ✅ `Since April, for about four months.`
     - `sq-2` (`single_choice`): `When did Kevin move into his studio flat nearby?` → ✅ `Two weeks ago.`
     - `sq-3` (`true_false`): `Kevin still works at the travel office now.` → `false`（听辨 `worked there for three years` + `left ... last winter` 已结束状态）。

8. **`translation` #1（L3 中译英 `zh_to_en`：一题联检 E1 / E2 / E3 / E4）**
   - **`source_text`**：`"我住在这个小区三年了，并且从去年九月起就在一家设计公司工作，但我是两周前才认识我邻居的。"`
   - **`reference_translation`**：`"I've lived in this neighborhood for three years, and I've worked at a design company since last September, but I met my neighbor two weeks ago."`
   - **`acceptable_alternates`**：`"I have lived in this neighborhood for three years and worked at a design company since last September, but I met my neighbor two weeks ago."` / `"I've lived in this community for three years, and I've worked in a design company since last September, though I met my neighbor two weeks ago."`

9. **`writing` #1（L4 造句 `subtype: "sentence_making"`：个人履历与双时态对比输出）**
   - **任务要求（`requirements`）**：用 2–3 句英文（25–50 词）做简短自我介绍：① 用**现在完成时 + `for`** 写 1 项至今持续的状态；② 用**现在完成时 + `since`** 写 1 项从某起点延续至今的状态；③ 用**一般过去时 + `yesterday / last ... / ... ago`** 补写 1 个已结束细节。禁在持续状态用一般现在时，禁将完成时与 `ago / last ...` 混用。
   - **范文与 `sample_response_tts`**：`sample_response`: `"I have lived in Hangzhou for four years, and I have worked in customer support since last March.    - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：① `时态与时间标志准确（持续至今用完成时、已结束用过去时，无 E1/E3 错误）`（40 分）；② `for / since 搭配与动词形态准确（时段配 for、起点配 since、V2/V3 拼写无误）`（35 分）；③ `语境连贯与篇幅达标（表达自然、标点规范、满足词数与要点）`（25 分）。

### 配套口语微纠偏练习（Speaking Micro-Drills）
1. **时间卡一秒换挡**：看短语即刻造整句——`three years` → `I've lived here for three years.`；`last May` → `I've lived here since last May.`；`three years ago` → `I moved here three years ago.`。
2. **`How long` 到 `When` 双轮快切**：演练 `How long have you worked here? → For two years. → When did you start? → Two years ago.`，固化开场完成时、追问切过去时的节奏。
3. **瞬间动词换装跟读**：成对朗读 `I bought this phone two years ago.` = `I've had this phone for two years.`；`She joined the team last April.` = `She's been on the team since last April.`。

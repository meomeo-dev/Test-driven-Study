## 一、中文母语（L1）六大高频写作负迁移偏误谱系

汉语靠时间词表时态，副词惯常置于动词前，且常用逗号串句与连续主语平铺。A2 学习者撰写 60–80 词阶段总结或工作周报时，失分集中于以下 6 类偏误：

| 编号 | 偏误类别 | 中文负迁移根源 | 典型病句（❌） | 地道修正（✅） | 优先级 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **跨时间轴时态切换混乱** | 仅凭“本周/昨天/下周”区分时间，动词不随锚点换挡；或把完成时误配已结束的过去时间点 | `*So far, I finished two units. Yesterday I have sent the report. Next week I share the slides.` / `*I study English for two months.` | `So far, I have finished two units. Yesterday, I sent the report. Next week, I will share the slides.` / `I have studied English for two months.` | **P0** |
| **E2** | **`already / yet` 漏用、极性混用与语序错位** | 汉语“已经/还没”固定在动词前，导致 `already` 错放 `have` 前或误入否定句、`yet` 前置，或漏 `yet` 缺失“待办”信号 | `*I already have sent the notes.` / `*I haven't already checked the data.` / `*I yet haven't started Unit 14.` / `*I didn't finish the draft.`（汇报待办漏完成视角） | `I have already sent the notes.` / `I haven't checked the data yet.` / `I haven't started Unit 14 yet.` / `I haven't finished the draft yet.` | **P0** |
| **E3** | **“我……我……”流水账与“一逗到底”黏连句** | 日记式逐条平铺，连续重复 `I` 开头、缺乏衔接；或用逗号直接拼接两个完整主谓句 | `*This week I finished Unit 12. I did the test. I wrote notes. I haven't read the email, I will read it tonight.` | `So far this week, I have finished Unit 12 and reviewed my notes. I haven't read the email yet, so I will read it tonight.` | **P0** |
| **E4** | **进行中状态与完成/一般态混用** | 汉语“还在等/还在写”无进行态屈折，常漏 `be + V-ing` 或误拼 `*have + V-ing` | `*I still wait for Maya's reply.` / `*I have working on the slide deck.` | `I am still waiting for Maya's reply.` / `I am still working on the slide deck.` | **P1** |
| **E5** | **切态中漏助动词或误用过去式代过去分词** | 多时态频繁切换时照中文“我已经做完”省略 `have/has`，或把 `wrote / took` 当过去分词 | `*I already finish the summary.` / `*She has wrote the weekly update.` | `I have already finished the summary.` / `She has written the weekly update.` | **P1** |
| **E6** | **进展高频中式错搭（不可数与动宾直译）** | 直译“取得一个大进步 / 做进步 / 给周进度”，给不可数名词 `progress` 加 `a` 或复数 | `*I made a big progress.` / `*We did good progresses.` / `*Here is my weekly progress.`（指周报邮件） | `I have made great progress.` / `We have made good progress.` / `Here is my weekly update.` | **P1** |

## 二、三大核心易混辨析矩阵（写前精准防踩坑）

### 1. 阶段汇报“四态分工”切换表（专治 E1 / E4）

依据 *British Council LearnEnglish (A2 Grammar & Writing)*，进度短文的关键在于**随时间锚点果断换挡**（不引入 B1 现在完成进行时）：

| 汇报板块 | 目标时态 | 典型时间锚点 / 信号词 | 地道 A2 示范句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **阶段成果与待办（至今）** | 现在完成时 `have/has + V3` | `so far`, `this week`, `already`, `not ... yet`, `for/since` | `So far this week, I have already finished the draft, but I haven't checked the numbers yet.` |
| **关键过去节点（已结束）** | 一般过去时 `V-ed / V2` | `yesterday`, `on Tuesday`, `last Friday`, `two days ago` | `Yesterday, I met our designer and confirmed the new layout.` |
| **当前推进与等待（此刻）** | 现在进行时 `am/is/are + V-ing` | `right now`, `still`, `at the moment` | `Right now, I am still waiting for the event dates from the team.` |
| **下一步计划与承诺（将来）** | `be going to + V` / `will + V` | `tomorrow`, `next week`, `by Friday`, `soon` | `Next Monday, I am going to update the link and send the final file by Tuesday.` |

> **切换红线**：① 美式口语偶见 `I already sent it`，但 CEFR A2 书面阶段汇报呼应 `so far / this week` 时**统一优先使用 `have/has already + V3`**，与 `yesterday / last week` 的一般过去时界线分明；② 遇到已结束具体过去时间点必须立刻切一般过去时。

### 2. `already` / `yet` / `still` 语序与极性辨析（专治 E2 / E4）

依据 *Cambridge Dictionary (English Grammar Today)*，三词在进度更新中的句型与位置严格分工：

| 副词 | 常用句型 | 标准位置 | 语用信号 | 正误对照 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`already`** | 肯定句（书面常规） | **中置**：`have/has + already + V3`（句尾仅用于口语表强烈惊讶） | 成果已提前或如期完成 | ✅ `We have already booked the room.`<br>❌ `*We already have booked...` / `*We haven't already booked...` |
| **`yet`** | 否定句、疑问句 | **严格句尾**：`haven't/hasn't + V3 + ... + yet` | 眼下未完，但仍在计划内 | ✅ `I haven't updated the slides yet.`<br>❌ `*I yet haven't updated...` / `*I haven't yet the slides.` |
| **`still`** | 进行肯定句 / 强语气否定句 | **中置**：`be + still + V-ing`；否定句放 `haven't` **之前**（`still haven't + V3`） | 正在推进，或强调至今仍卡住 | ✅ `I am still working on Unit 13.` / `They still haven't replied.`<br>❌ `*I haven't still replied.` / `*I still wait...` |

- **口诀**：**肯定 `already` 夹中间，否定 `yet` 放句尾，进行 `still` 跟 `be` 后**。

### 3. 流水账升级“四招工具箱”与搭配纠偏（专治 E3 / E6）

严守第 15 讲前已学语法边界（仅用 `and / but / so`、同主语并列省略与句首时间锚点，不提前透支第 19 讲 `although / however`）：

| 优化策略 | 流水账原句 | A2 升级示范（✅） | 效果 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **① 同主语并列合并** | `I have finished Unit 12. I have reviewed the notes.` | `I have finished Unit 12 and reviewed the notes.` | 消重复 `I have...` |
| **② 已完/未完 `but` 对举** | `I have written the report. I haven't sent it.` | `I have already written the report, but I haven't sent it yet.` | 一句讲清进展与缺口 |
| **③ 现状接 `so` 引计划** | `I haven't finished the quiz yet. I will do it tonight.` | `I haven't finished the quiz yet, so I will do it tonight.` | 形成待办→行动闭环 |
| **④ 句首时间锚点领起** | 全篇 `I did... I met... I will...` 开头 | `So far this week, ... / Yesterday, ... / Next Monday, ...` | 骨架清晰，节奏立体 |

- **不可数与动宾提醒**：`progress` 不可数，固定搭配 `make (good/great) progress`，禁写 `*a progress / *progresses / *do progress`；“发周报”用 `send a weekly update`，“推进任务”用 `work on + 任务`。

## 三、写后“四步红笔自检法”与三组诊改示范（对齐 `min_examples: 3`）

**30 秒四步红笔法**：① **核锚点与时态**（`So far` 完成、`yesterday` 过去、`still` 进行、`next week` 将来，查过去分词）；② **核 `already / yet / still` 位置**；③ **清流水账与黏连句**（用 `and / but / so` 合并连续 `I... I...`，拆逗号串句）；④ **核搭配与 60–80 词篇幅**。

### 例题 1：远程办公周报诊改（纠偏 E1–E6，64 词 → 70 词）
- **原病文（❌，64 词）**：
  > *Hi Sarah, This week I finished the user survey. Yesterday I have talked with the design team about the homepage. I already have sent the notes to Leo. I didn't update the slide deck, I still wait for the new photos. I made a big progress this week. Next Monday I prepare the final version. Next Tuesday I share it with everyone. Best, Lin*
- **诊断**：`Yesterday I have talked` 过去时间误配完成时；`already have sent` 语序倒置；`didn't update...` 缺完成时与 `yet` 且逗号黏连，`still wait` 漏进行时；`a big progress` 不可数错；`Next Monday/Tuesday` 漏将来时且重复主语。
- **高分改写（✅，70 词）**：
  > *Hi Sarah, So far this week, I have already finished the user survey and sent the notes to Leo, so we have made great progress. Yesterday, I met the design team to discuss the homepage. I am still waiting for the new photos, and I haven't updated the slide deck yet. Next Monday, I am going to prepare the final version and share it with everyone on Tuesday. Best, Lin*

### 例题 2：英语学习小结“去流水账”升级（54 词 → 70 词）
- **原流水账（❌，54 词）**：
  > *I study English for two months. This month I finished Unit 12. I finished the listening test. Yesterday I have learned 15 new words. I yet haven't started Unit 14. I haven't already written my weekly note. Tomorrow I review my mistakes. Next week I start the new unit. I did a good progress.*
- **诊断**：9 句有 6 句以 `I` 开头；`study ... for two months` 漏完成时；`Yesterday I have learned` 时态倒挂；`yet haven't` 与 `haven't already` 极性语序双错；`did a good progress` 搭配错；`Tomorrow / Next week` 漏将来时。
- **高分改写（✅，70 词）**：
  > *I have studied English for two months and made good progress. So far this month, I have already finished Unit 12 and passed the listening test. Yesterday, I learned fifteen new words and reviewed my notes. Right now, I am still working on the speaking exercises, and I haven't started Unit 14 yet. Tomorrow, I am going to review my mistakes, and next week I will begin the new unit.*

### 例题 3：三组高频“单句病点 + 流水账”快改
1. **过去节点混完成时 + `yet` 前置**：`*Last Wednesday I have met my tutor, and I yet haven't finished the reading.` → `Last Wednesday, I met my tutor, but I haven't finished the reading yet.`
2. **漏 `have` + 不可数 `progress`**：`*We already check the budget and made a great progress.` → `We have already checked the budget and made great progress.`
3. **三句流水账合为进展链**：`*I finished part one. I still write part two. I send it tomorrow.` → `I have already finished Part One, and I am still writing Part Two, so I will send it tomorrow.`

## 四、听说读写练习设计蓝图（对齐 `budget_plan.json` 8 题配额）

配额共 **8 题**（`single_choice ×2`、`true_false ×1`、`cloze ×1`、`translation ×2`、`writing: sentence_making ×1`、`writing: short_essay ×1`），分三阶递进并补齐听/读/说微闭环：

### 1. L1 单点诊断（3 题）

1. **`single_choice` #1（时态切换与 `already / yet` 语序，含病句全组禁配 TTS）**
   - **题干**：撰写英文工作周报时，哪一句的**时态切换与 `already / yet` 语序均完全正确**？
   - **选项**：
     - ✅ `So far this week, I have already sent the schedule, and yesterday I met the client online.`
     - ❌ `So far this week, I already have sent the schedule, and yesterday I have met the client online.`（`already` 前置 + `yesterday` 误配完成时）
     - ❌ `So far this week, I sent the schedule, and I yet haven't called the client.`（`So far` 漏完成时 + `yet` 前置）
     - ❌ `So far this week, I have sent the schedule, and I haven't already called the client.`（否定句误用 `already`）
2. **`single_choice` #2（“去流水账”句式整合与 `progress` 搭配，含病句全组禁配 TTS）**
   - **题干**：把“完成了第三章、还没做听力练习、今晚打算补上”整合为流畅小结，哪一句**语法准确且无流水账**？
   - **选项**：
     - ✅ `I have already finished Chapter 3, but I haven't done the listening practice yet, so I will do it tonight.`
     - ❌ `I finished Chapter 3, I didn't do the listening practice, I do it tonight.`（一逗到底 + 漏完成时/`yet`/将来时）
     - ❌ `I have finished Chapter 3 and made a big progress, but I haven't yet done the listening practice.`（`a big progress` 错 + `yet` 置中）
     - ❌ `I already finish Chapter 3, and I still wait to do the listening practice tonight.`（漏 `have` 与过去分词 + `still wait` 漏进行时）
3. **`true_false` #1（过去时间边界与 `yet` 位置，纯中文无 TTS）**
   - **题干**：判断正误：写英语阶段总结时，只要强调某事“已完成”，即使句中有 `yesterday` 或 `last Friday`，也必须用 `have already + 过去分词`；且否定句中 `yet` 放 `haven't` 与动词之间。
   - **判定与解析**：`false`。具体过去时间（`yesterday / last Friday`）须切一般过去时；`have already + V3` 汇报不指明过去时刻的阶段成果，且 `yet` 严格置于否定句和疑问句**句尾**。

### 2. L2 语篇完形与双向互译（3 题）

4. **`cloze` #1（职场周五进度邮件 5 空完形；`passage` 禁内联 `tts`，复盘配 `passage_tts`，5 空选项全组无 TTS）**
   - **语篇（65 词）**：*"Hi Mia, Here is my weekly update on the website project. So far, I have {{1}} finished the new menu page, and we have made great {{2}} with the layout. Yesterday, I {{3}} the writer to confirm the text. I am still working on the help page, and I haven't checked the links {{4}}. Next Monday, I {{5}} the final draft with the team."*
   - **正解与干扰项**：`{{1}}` ✅ `already`（❌ `yet / ever / ago`）；`{{2}}` ✅ `progress`（❌ `a progress / progresses / an update`）；`{{3}}` ✅ `called`（❌ `have called / call / am calling`）；`{{4}}` ✅ `yet`（❌ `already / still / just`）；`{{5}}` ✅ `will share`（❌ `share / shared / have shared`）。
5. **`translation` #1（中译英 `zh_to_en`，配 `reference_translation_tts`，仅复盘/背题可见）**
   - **原文**：「到目前为止，我已经发了本周总结，但还没整理会议笔记，所以我明天上午会做完。」
   - **参考译文**：`So far, I have already sent the weekly summary, but I haven't organized the meeting notes yet, so I will finish them tomorrow morning.`（接受 `sorted / updated`、`am going to finish` 或两句拆分；重点纠偏 `*already have sent`、`*yet haven't` 与漏将来时）。
6. **`translation` #2（英译中 `en_to_zh`，为 `source_text` 配 `source_text_tts`，作答前可见）**
   - **原文**：`I have studied this course for three weeks and made good progress. Last Tuesday, I passed the Unit 12 quiz, and I am still reviewing the new words. Next week, I am going to start my writing practice.`
   - **参考译文**：「我已经学习这门课程三周了，取得了不错的进步。上周二我通过了第十二单元测试，目前还在复习生词。下周我打算开始写作练习。」

### 3. L3 病句升级与阶段短文写作（2 题）

7. **`writing`（`sentence_making`）#1（病句诊改与“去流水账”合并；配 `sample_response_tts`）**
   - **任务**：将下面 3 句病句改写并整合为 **2 句连贯自然、28–42 词**的学习进度更新：

     - ② `*So far, I already have reviewed my notes, I yet haven't done the quiz.`

8. **`writing`（`short_essay`）#1（60–80 词阶段总结或远程办公周报）**
   - **任务**：二选一写 **60–80 词**短文：① 本周远程办公项目进度周报；② 近两周个人英语学习打卡小结。须覆盖“已完成 → 过去节点 → 进行/待办 → 下一步计划”，混用完成/过去/将来时，规范使用 `already` 与句尾 `yet` 各至少 1 次，并用 `and / but / so` 与句首时间锚点消除流水账。
   - **参考范文（73 词，职场周报）**：`Hi Alex, Here is my weekly update on the May newsletter. So far, I have already finished the main article and chosen three photos, so we have made good progress. Yesterday, I met our designer to check the layout. I am still waiting for the event dates, and I haven't added the sign-up link yet. Next Monday, I am going to update the link and send the final version by Tuesday. Best, Lin`
   - **评分细则（`rubric`，总权 100）**：① 要素完整且 60–80 词（25 分）；② 四态切换、过去分词与进行态准确（25 分）；③ `already / yet` 语序与进展搭配准确（25 分）；④ 衔接自然、无流水账与黏连句（25 分）。

### 4. 配套听、读、说微纠偏闭环
- **听（锚词+句尾 `yet` 辨听）**：跟听 `translation` #2 与周报范文，首遍专记时间锚词（`So far / Yesterday / still / Next Monday`），次遍核对动词变位与句尾轻读 `yet`。

## 五、生词本 2 词推荐与命题审计红线（对齐 `vocab_target: 2`）

1. **生词本首选 2 词**（已核对 `courses/beginner-english/assets/vocabulary/english.json` 现有 93 词及第 13 单元前 4 讲候选词无 ID 重复；遵 `design-course-vocabulary` 仅 `word` 填 `pos`、`grammar_note <= 120` 字、词句纯净省略 `term_tts / example_tts`）：
   - **`progress`**（首选 #1）：`category: "word"`｜`pos: "n"`｜`meaning: "进展；进步"`｜`grammar_note: "不可数名词，不可加 a 或变复数（禁写 *a progress / *progresses）；高频搭配为 make progress，不与 do 搭配。"`｜`example: "We have made good progress on the website layout this week."`（`这周我们在网站排版上取得了不错的进展。`）
   - **`work-on`**（首选 #2）：`category: "phrase"`（省略 `pos`）｜`meaning: "从事；正在推进（某项任务或项目）"`｜`grammar_note: "及物动词短语，后直接接任务名词（work on the report）；汇报中常与 still + 进行时连用表“还在推进中”。"`｜`example: "I am still working on the weekly summary and will send it tomorrow."`（`我还在写周报总结，明天发给大家。`）

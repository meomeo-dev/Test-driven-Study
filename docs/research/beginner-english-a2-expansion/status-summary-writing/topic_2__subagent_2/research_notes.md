# 第15讲「阶段总结与进度汇报写作」范文拆解、进展短语库与情景语料笔记

## 1. 课程定位与 A2 语料边界

- **所属定位**：`beginner-english` 第 13 单元「个人经历与进度追踪（`experiences-progress`）」→ 第 15 讲「阶段总结与进度汇报写作（`status-summary-writing`）」。保留第 1–10 单元（37.33 小时、41 知识点）及既有 ID 不变，弱化车间背景，按 CEFR A1+→A2 体系扩展第 11–16 单元（+23.00 小时、+30 微知识点，共 60.33 小时）。
- **级别与配额（对齐 `budget_plan.json`）**：CEFR **A2**，`writing_application`，45 分钟（`target_read_units: 2180`，`min_examples: 3`）；配额：`writing (short_essay) × 1`、`writing (sentence_making) × 1`、`cloze × 1`、`translation × 2`、`single_choice × 2`、`true_false × 1`，`vocab_target: 2`。
- **先修与语法边界**：承接 `for-since-duration` 与 `progress-update-listening`，综合运用现在完成时（`have/has + done` + `so far / already / just / yet / for / since`）、一般过去时、现在进行时与将来时（`will / be going to`）。场景限定为**远程办公周报**与**日常语言学习打卡**（严禁车间/工位术语），不超前引入过去进行时、最高级/`too...to`、`should/have to/might`、`although/however`、条件句、被动语态或 B1 现在完成进行时。

## 2. 两篇 A2 高分示范短文（60–80 词）与四维拆解

### 范文 1：远程办公周报更新（74 词，现代远程职场场景）

> Hi team, here is my weekly update. So far, I have already finished the new user guide and sent out the monthly newsletter. On Tuesday, I met with the design team online and fixed three small errors on our website. Right now, I am still working on the client slides, and I have not checked the final notes yet. Next week, I will finish the slides on Monday and follow up with our clients.

- **中文参考译文**：团队大家好，这是我的本周进度更新。到目前为止，我已经完成了新版用户指南，并发出了月度简报。周二，我与设计团队在线开了会，修复了网站上的三处小错误。现在我仍在制作客户演示文稿，也还没核对最终笔记。下周我会在周一完成演示文稿，并跟进联系我们的客户。

| 结构要素 | 范文原句 | 时态分工与地道搭配亮点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **开场点题** | *Hi team, here is my weekly update.* | 远程团队群聊/周报标准起手式，简明直入主题。 |
| **已完成成果** | ***So far**, I **have already finished** the new user guide and **sent out** the monthly newsletter.* | `So far,` + 现在完成时汇总阶段产出；共用助动词 `have` 串联两个过去分词 `finished` 与 `sent out`（发出、群发）。 |
| **过去具体节点** | ***On Tuesday**, I **met with** the design team online and **fixed** three small errors on our website.* | 出现具体过去时间 `On Tuesday,`，果断切回一般过去时 `met with` / `fixed`；`online` 凸显远程协作语境。 |
| **进行中与未完项** | ***Right now**, I **am still working on** the client slides, and I **have not checked** the final notes **yet**.* | 现在进行时 `am still working on` 报进行中项；现在完成时否定 `have not ... yet` 报待办项，`yet` 置句末。 |
| **下一步计划** | ***Next week**, I **will finish** the slides on Monday and **follow up with** our clients.* | `Next week,` 零介词引出下周安排；`will` 表达对团队的明确承诺，`follow up with + 人` 表后续跟进联系。 |

> **邮件正式版微调提示**：若发给直属经理，将开头改为 *Dear Lisa, I am writing to share my weekly update.*，结尾补 *Please let me know if you have any questions. Best regards, Daniel*；若想强调持续投入，可将首句扩展为 *I have worked on the website update since Monday.*。

### 范文 2：语言学习打卡阶段总结（73 词，日常生活与自学场景）

> I have studied English in this group for four weeks, and I have made good progress. So far, I have already finished ten listening lessons and learned eighty new words. Yesterday, I went over my study notes and wrote a short weekly log. I have not practiced phone conversations yet, because I am still working on fast speech. Next week, I am going to review my vocabulary list and record two speaking updates.

- **中文参考译文**：我已经在这个小组学了四周英语，取得了不错的进步。到目前为止，我已经完成了十节听力课，学会了八十个新词。昨天我复习了学习笔记，写了一篇简短的周总结。我还没练习电话对话，因为我还在攻克快语速。下周，我打算复习生词表并录制两条口语打卡更新。

| 结构要素 | 范文原句 | 时态分工与地道搭配亮点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **持续时长＋总评** | *I **have studied** English in this group **for four weeks**, and I **have made good progress**.* | `for + 时间段` 配现在完成时交代打卡周期；核心搭配 `make good progress`（`progress` 不可数，不加 `a`）。 |
| **量化成果** | ***So far**, I **have already finished** ten listening lessons and **learned** eighty new words.* | 量化课数与词汇量，避免空洞流水账；`already` 置于 `have` 与过去分词之间。 |
| **近期关键动作** | ***Yesterday**, I **went over** my study notes and **wrote** a short weekly log.* | `Yesterday,` 触发一般过去时；进展短语 `go over`（复习、核查）过去式为 `went over`。 |
| **待办与卡点说明** | *I **have not practiced** phone conversations **yet**, because I **am still working on** fast speech.* | `haven't / have not ... yet` 交代未完成项，`because + 进行时` 自然说明当前专注攻克的难点。 |
| **下阶段计划** | ***Next week**, I **am going to review** my vocabulary list and **record** two speaking updates.* | `be going to + 动词原形` 表达下周既定学习打算，与 `and` 并列两个动词原形（省略重复 `am going to`）。 |

> **时间起点替换提示**：若改用时间起点，可将 `for four weeks` 替换为 `since May` 或 `since last month`；若在语言班提交作业，可自然嵌入 `I handed in my writing task on Thursday.`（周四交了写作作业）。

## 3. 常用进展动词短语库与写作替换模块

### 3.1 常用进展动词短语与搭配库（按推进阶段分组）

| 阶段 | 英文短语 / 搭配 | 中文释义 | 语法属性与三态 / 介词要点 | A2 地道例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **推进中** | `work on + sth.` | 着手处理；正在做 | **不可分离**（`work on it` ✓，禁 ~~work it on~~）；规则变化 `worked` | *Right now, I **am still working on** the client slides.* |
| **推进中** | `make (good / steady) progress` | 取得（良好/稳步）进展 | `progress` **不可数**，禁 `a progress` 或 `-s`；接 `in / with`；`made` | *We **have made steady progress** with the new website.* |
| **推进中** | `keep up with + sth.` | 跟上进度；不掉队 | **三词不可分**；`keep - kept - kept` | *I read twenty minutes a day to **keep up with** the course.* |
| **推进中** | `stay on track / on schedule` | 保持按计划推进 | 常接 `for + 截止日`（延续第 14 讲 `on track`） | *We **are staying on track** for Monday's launch.* |
| **核查收尾** | `go over + sth.` | 仔细复查；复习笔记 | **不可分离**（`go over it` ✓）；不规则 `go - went - gone` | *Yesterday, I **went over** my study notes before bed.* |
| **核查收尾** | `send out + sth.` | 发出；群发（邮件/简报） | **可分离**，代词中置（`send it out` ✓）；`send - sent - sent` | *I **have already sent out** the weekly update to the team.* |
| **核查收尾** | `hand in + sth.` | 提交（报告/作业） | **可分离**，代词中置（`hand it in` ✓）；规则变化 `handed` | *She **handed in** her writing summary on Thursday.* |
| **核查收尾** | `wrap up + sth.` | 圆满收尾；结束（任务/会） | **可分离**（`wrap it up` ✓）；双写 `p`：`wrapped up` | *I will **wrap up** the design notes before five o'clock.* |
| **核查收尾** | `check off + sth.` | 在清单上勾掉已完成项 | **可分离**（`check them off` ✓）；规则变化 `checked` | *I **have checked off** four tasks on my list today.* |
| **卡点跟进** | `wait for + sb./sth.` | 等待某人/某回复 | 不可漏 `for`；常配现在进行时 `still waiting for` | *I **am still waiting for** Leo's reply on the budget.* |
| **卡点跟进** | `fall behind (on sth.)` | 进度落后 | 不规则 `fall - fell - fallen`；反义 `keep up with` | *I missed two classes, so I **fell behind** on listening.* |
| **卡点跟进** | `follow up (with sb. / on sth.)` | 跟进联系 / 跟进事项 | **人用 `with`，事用 `on`**；规则变化 `followed up` | *Next week, I will **follow up with** our clients by email.* |
| **卡点跟进** | `catch up on + sth.` | 补上落下的功课/工作 | **三词不可分**（`catch up on it` ✓）；`catch - caught - caught` | *This weekend, I am going to **catch up on** my reading.* |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 4. 写作语境中的核心语法与搭配微笔记

1. **一篇短文的“四时态分工”**（避免通篇过去时流水账）：

| 信息模块 | 推荐时态 | 标志时间语 | 易错预警 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **阶段成果／时长** | 现在完成时 `have/has + V-pp` | `So far`, `already`, `for ...`, `since ...` | 禁配 `yesterday / on Tuesday` |
| **具体过去节点** | 一般过去时 `V-ed` / 不规则式 | `Yesterday`, `On Tuesday`, `Last Friday` | 禁写 ~~Yesterday I have finished~~ |
| **眼下进行／等待** | 现在进行时 `am/is/are + V-ing` | `Right now`, `still` | `work on` / `wait for` 禁漏介词 |
| **下步承诺／打算** | `will` / `be going to + V原形` | `Next week`, `On Monday`, `This weekend` | `Next week` 前零介词 |

2. **进展短语“可分 vs 不可分”代词位**：
   - **可分离**（`send out` / `hand in` / `wrap up` / `check off`）：代词宾语**须放中间**（`send it out` ✓，禁 ~~send out it~~ ✗）；名词前后皆可。
   - **不可分离**（`work on` / `go over` / `wait for` / `keep up with` / `catch up on`）：名词和代词**一律后置**（`work on it` ✓、`go over them` ✓、`catch up on it` ✓）。
3. **三大高频搭配排雷**：
   - **`make progress`**：`progress` 不可数，只写 `make good/steady progress`，禁 ~~make a progress~~ 或 ~~progresses~~。
   - **`follow up` 介词**：跟进**人**用 `with`（`follow up with clients`），跟进**事**用 `on`（`follow up on the email`）。
   - **`catch up` 辨义**：亲友 `catch up (with sb.)` 表“叙旧”（第 14 讲）；本讲 `catch up on + 任务/课程` 表“补进度”。
4. **语域与标点**：周报与学习总结属半正式书面语，通篇宜保持一致（范文用 `have already` / `have not ... yet` 强化语序，口语对话自然用 `I've / haven't / I'm / I'll`）。句首时间词 `So far,`、`On Tuesday,`、`Next week,` 后加逗号独立成句，避免逗号拼接两句。

## 6. 题库配额锚点与零冲突生词本推荐

### 6.1 对齐 `budget_plan.json` 的题型落点

| 题型与配额 | 推荐考查任务与参考答案要点 |
| :--- | :--- |
| `writing (short_essay) × 1` | **60–80 词短文写作**：远程办公周报或学习打卡阶段总结；须含已完成（现在完成时）、过去节点（一般过去时）、进行中/未完成（`still working on` / `haven't ... yet`）、下周计划（`will` / `be going to`）及至少 2 个进展短语。范文取第 2 节范文 1（拓展范文 2）；`sample_response` / `sample_response_tts` 仅复盘与背题可见。 |
| `writing (sentence_making) × 1` | **两句微汇报**：第 1 句现在完成时报已完成与未完成项，第 2 句用 `will` 写下周跟进（含 `send out` / `follow up with`）。范句：*So far, I have already sent out the report, but I have not checked the final notes yet. Next week, I will finish the slides and follow up with our clients.*（`sample_response_tts` 仅复盘/背题可见）。 |
| `cloze × 1` | 下附 **66 词** 远程博客更新（6 空：`for`、`made`、`already`、`went`、`yet`、`will`）；`passage_tts` 仅复盘/背题可见。 |
| `translation × 2` | ①（`zh_to_en`）到目前为止，我已经发出了月度简报，但还没核对最终笔记。→ *So far, I have already sent out the monthly newsletter, but I have not checked the final notes yet.*<br>②（`zh_to_en`）昨天我复习了学习笔记，下周我打算复习生词表。→ *Yesterday, I went over my study notes, and next week I am going to review my vocabulary list.*（`reference_translation_tts` 仅复盘/背题可见）。 |
| `single_choice × 2` | ① 不可数搭配：*I have studied English in this group for four weeks, and I have ______.*（正答：`made good progress`；干扰项：`made a good progress` / `make good progress` / `made good progresses`）；<br>② 代词与介词：*The user guide is ready. I will ______ to the team and ______ our clients on Monday.*（正答：`send it out; follow up with`；干扰项：`send out it; follow up with` / `send it out; follow up on` / `sent it out; follow up`）。 |
| `true_false × 1` | 表达“昨天我复习了学习笔记并修改了三处错误”写作 *Yesterday, I have gone over my study notes and fixed three errors.* 是正确的。（**False**，`Yesterday` 须用一般过去时 `went over`）。 |

- **完形填空（`cloze × 1`）示范短文（66 词，6 空，TTS 纯净）**：
  - **挖空版**：> I have worked on our remote team blog __(1)__ [for] three weeks, and we have __(2)__ [made] steady progress. So far, I have __(3)__ [already] written four short posts and sent them out to our readers. Yesterday, I __(4)__ [went] over the comments and fixed two spelling mistakes. I have not finished the photo page __(5)__ [yet]. Next week, I __(6)__ [will] wrap up the page and follow up with our editor.
  - **完整朗读稿（`passage_tts`，仅复盘与背题可见）**：> I have worked on our remote team blog for three weeks, and we have made steady progress. So far, I have already written four short posts and sent them out to our readers. Yesterday, I went over the comments and fixed two spelling mistakes. I have not finished the photo page yet. Next week, I will wrap up the page and follow up with our editor.
  - **参考译文**：我已经负责我们远程团队的博客三周了，我们取得了稳步进展。到目前为止，我已经写了四篇短文并发给了读者。昨天我复查了留言，修改了两处拼写错误。我还没完成图片页面。下周我会把这个页面收尾，并跟进联系我们的编辑。

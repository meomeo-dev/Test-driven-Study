Learning target and evidence
Identify one decision or skill the learner should be able to repeat on a new
item. Record the decisive condition and the supplied evidence that supports it.
Use the passage, transcript, diagram or stated values for item-specific facts.
For a numeric item, record the quantity, unit and an exact calculation.

Retrieval and gap checks
Use search to verify the general method, definition or rule that explains the
decision. Prefer a primary or authoritative teaching source. Check which
conditions and scope the source supports, including the version or effective
date when it matters. A missing external copy of the item is not an evidence gap.

Handoff
Return brief notes with the learning target, decisive item evidence and its
location, the external teaching claim and retrieved source URL, and any
material conflict or gap. Label a plausible learner mistake as an inference.

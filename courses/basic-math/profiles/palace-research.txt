Establish the quantities
Identify the whole that represents 1 or 100%, the unit, the part or ratio
being compared, and the requested answer form. Recompute the answer from the
item before reading the explanation. Check each candidate independently on
multiple-choice items. Keep exact values unless the item requests rounding.

Check the mathematical rule
Use a reputable educational source for the specific rule or representation.
For a fraction, verify what the numerator and denominator count and that the
parts are equal. For a decimal, check the place values and the effect of a
trailing zero. For a percent, express the amount per hundred and name its
reference whole. Use the stated original value as the denominator when the
item asks for relative change. Keep exact equality and approximation distinct.

Return evidence for planning
Give the whole and unit, a short exact calculation with the required answer
form, the decisive rule with a retrieved URL and the condition it supports.
Name a likely distractor error, such as using a different whole, shifting one
place instead of two, or treating a trailing zero as a change of value. Mark
an inferred misconception as such. Flag a bank answer or source that conflicts
with the calculation and identify the disputed quantity or condition.

Decision
Name one transferable judgment. Identify the condition that makes it true and
verify it against the supplied item. For a transcript or passage, locate the
decisive words. For a calculation, preserve the quantity, unit and exact value.
Identify the nearest plausible mistake and the check that prevents it.

Scene design
Use the composition and style from the course image template. Choose one
scene, diagram or necessary comparison that makes the decisive condition
visible. In anchors, pair each visible cue with the judgment it supports.
Add one brief recall check in scene that the learner can answer from the image.

Output checks
Write image_prompt as direct drawing instructions. State the objects, their
positions or quantities, and the exact short labels allowed on the image.
Verify each number and quotation against the supplied evidence.
With search enabled, use supported when the item evidence establishes the
answer and retrieved sources support the general teaching claim. A missing
external copy is not an evidence gap.
